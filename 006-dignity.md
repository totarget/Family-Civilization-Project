# 006 | Dignity / 尊严

## English Definition

Human dignity is intrinsic.

It does not come from wealth, power, status, achievement, nationality, usefulness, obedience, or social approval.

A civilized society is one that recognizes the inherent worth of every person, including the weak, the poor, the different, the wounded, and the powerless.

## 中文定义

人的尊严，是天然存在的。

它不来自财富、权力、地位、成就、国籍、用途、服从或社会认可。

真正文明的社会，会承认每一个人天然的价值，包括弱者、贫者、异类、受伤者以及没有权力的人。
