# 021 | Morality / 道德

## English Definition

Morality is not merely social rules, tradition, or obedience to what others demand.

Within many traditional civilizations, morality was often used as a tool for maintaining order, enforcing obedience, suppressing individuality, and preserving authority or power.

As a result, many people grew up associating morality not with dignity, kindness, freedom, or respect, but with guilt, pressure, shame, suppression, and fear of disobedience.

True morality should never require the destruction of human dignity, boundaries, or authentic selfhood.

Within modern relationship civilization, healthy morality should reduce suffering, protect human dignity, strengthen mutual respect, and help people live more freely, meaningfully, and humanely.

The true measure of morality is not how obedient it makes people. It is whether it reduces human suffering and whether it truly treats human beings as ends in themselves rather than merely as means.

## 中文定义

“道德”，并不只是社会规则、传统规范，或者“别人要求你做什么”。

在很多旧文明结构中，道德长期被当作维持秩序、要求服从、群体压制个体、权威控制关系的工具。

因此，很多人一听到“道德”，想到的不是尊重、善良、自由，而是愧疚、压力、评判与控制。

真正的道德，不应建立在人格压迫、边界破坏或真实自我消失之上。

在现代关系文明中，健康的道德应该帮助人与人之间减少伤害，建立尊重，保护人格，并让人活得更自由、更有尊严、更幸福。

衡量一种道德是否文明，不在于它让人多么服从，而在于它是否减少了人的痛苦，以及它是否真正把人当作目的，而不是手段。
