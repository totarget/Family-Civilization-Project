# 022 | Family Authority Mechanism / 家庭权力机制

## English Definition

A family authority mechanism is the way a family defines where authority comes from, how decisions are made, how rules are created, and how power is limited inside intimate relationships.

In a traditional family structure, authority is often assumed to come from blood, age, parental status, economic contribution, sacrifice, or seniority. Under this logic, the parent, elder, or resource-holder is believed to possess the natural right to command, control, and define the child.

The Family Civilization Project rejects this logic.

Blood is not a source of domination. Birth is not ownership. Caregiving is not a license to control. Economic support is not permission to erase another person’s dignity.

A civilized family authority mechanism must be built on shared rules, mutual respect, clear boundaries, age-appropriate participation, responsibility, and the protection of human dignity.

In such a family, neither parents nor children rule absolutely. Shared rules rule. Parents remain guardians and guides, but their authority is constrained by dignity, boundaries, and the child’s independent personhood.

Children are not given unlimited power; they are gradually given voice, participation, choice, and responsibility according to their age and capacity.

The purpose of family authority is not to make children obey, but to help every family member learn responsibility, cooperation, negotiation, self-management, and respect for others.

A healthy family does not ask, “Who has the final power?” It asks, “What rules can protect everyone’s dignity and help this family live together with love, order, freedom, and responsibility?”

## 中文定义

家庭权力机制，是指一个家庭如何定义权力从哪里来、家庭决策如何形成、规则如何制定，以及亲密关系中的权力如何受到限制。

在传统家庭结构中，权力常常被默认来自血缘、年龄、父母身份、经济供养、牺牲或长辈资格。在这种逻辑下，父母、长辈或资源掌握者，被认为天然拥有命令、控制和定义孩子的权力。

家庭文明工程否定这种逻辑。

血缘不是支配权的来源。生育不是所有权。养育不是控制许可证。经济供养也不是抹去另一个人尊严的理由。

文明的家庭权力机制，必须建立在共同规则、彼此尊重、清晰边界、符合年龄的参与权、责任意识和人格尊严保护之上。

在这样的家庭里，既不是父母绝对说了算，也不是孩子绝对说了算，而是共同制定的规则说了算。父母仍然是守护者和引导者，但父母的权威必须受到尊严、边界和孩子独立人格的约束。

孩子也不是获得无限权力，而是随着年龄和能力，逐步获得表达权、参与权、选择权和责任。

家庭权力的目的，不是让孩子服从，而是帮助每一个家庭成员学习责任、协作、协商、自我管理和尊重他人。

一个健康家庭不应只问：“到底谁说了算？”而应问：“什么样的规则，能够保护每个人的尊严，并帮助这个家庭在爱、秩序、自由与责任中共同生活？”
