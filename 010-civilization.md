# 010 | Civilization / 文明

## English Definition

Civilization is not measured by technological power, military strength, economic scale, or architectural grandeur.

Civilization is measured by how human beings treat one another.

## 中文定义

文明，不取决于科技、军事、经济规模或高楼大厦。

文明真正的尺度是：人如何对待人。
