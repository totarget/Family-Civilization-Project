# 004 | Freedom / 自由

## English Definition

Freedom is not the absence of limits.

Freedom is the ability to consciously choose one’s own path, bear responsibility for one’s life, and live according to one’s authentic will without being reduced into obedience, fear, or manipulation.

Freedom without responsibility becomes chaos.

Responsibility without freedom becomes slavery.

## 中文定义

自由，不是毫无限制。

真正的自由，是一个人能够基于自己的真实意志，选择人生方向，承担人生责任，而不是活成恐惧、服从与操控之下的产物。

没有责任的自由，会变成混乱。

没有自由的责任，会变成奴役。
