# 015 | Civilization System / 文明系统

## English Definition

A mature civilization is not one that believes itself eternally correct.

A mature civilization is one that permanently preserves the ability to self-correct.

## 中文定义

成熟的文明，不是认为自己永远正确。

真正成熟的文明，是永远保留自我纠错能力的文明。
