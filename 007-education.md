# 007 | Education / 教育

## English Definition

Education is not the industrial production of obedient workers.

Education is the lifelong process through which a person develops selfhood, judgment, creativity, emotional maturity, moral awareness, and the ability to live freely and responsibly.

The highest goal of education is not utility.

It is human flourishing.

## 中文定义

教育，不是流水线式地制造服从型工具。

真正的教育，是帮助一个人发展主体性、判断力、创造力、情感成熟、道德意识，以及自由而负责地活着的能力。

教育的最高目标，不是“有用”。

而是人的完整成长。
