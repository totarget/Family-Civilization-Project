# 018 | Compassion / 慈悲

## English Definition

Compassion is not pity from a position of superiority.

True compassion is the inability to ignore the suffering of others after deeply understanding suffering oneself.

Compassion is not weakness, moral performance, emotional self-indulgence, self-erasure, or limitless tolerance.

True compassion begins with understanding human beings, understanding suffering, and understanding relational trauma.

Human beings often hurt others not because they are inherently evil, but because they themselves were once hurt, were never genuinely loved, never learned healthy relationships, or grew up within fear, control, and emotional oppression.

Therefore, compassion is not merely sympathy. Its deeper meaning is the desire to help build a civilization that produces less suffering.

Within the Family Civilization Project, compassion means respecting the dignity of children, respecting personal boundaries, refusing to turn love into control, refusing to turn education into oppression, and refusing to turn relationships into systems of power.

Compassion does not mean accepting everything without limits. It means that even after fully recognizing human fragility, one still chooses to help build a healthier, freer, and more loving civilization.

## 中文定义

“慈悲”，并不是高高在上的怜悯。

真正的慈悲是：当一个人真正理解痛苦之后，开始不忍心他人继续陷入同样的痛苦。

慈悲并不意味着软弱，也不是道德表演、自我感动、牺牲人格或无底线忍让。

真正的慈悲，首先建立在对人的理解、对痛苦的理解、对关系伤害的理解之上。

很多时候，人之所以伤害别人，并不是因为天生邪恶，而是因为自己也曾被伤害，从未真正被爱过，从未学会健康关系，长期活在控制与恐惧之中。

因此，真正的慈悲，不只是“同情”，更重要的是希望建立一种更少制造痛苦的人类文明。

在家庭文明工程中，慈悲意味着尊重孩子的人格，尊重人的边界，不把爱变成控制，不把教育变成压迫，不把关系变成权力结构。

慈悲不是纵容一切。而是即使理解人性的脆弱，依然努力推动一个更健康、更自由、更有爱的文明。
