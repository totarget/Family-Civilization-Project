# 005 | Happiness / 幸福

## English Definition

Happiness is not pleasure, comparison, status, consumption, or endless desire satisfaction.

Happiness is the long-term state in which a person can live truthfully as themselves while maintaining healthy relationships with others, the world, and their own inner life.

## 中文定义

幸福，不是快感，不是比较，不是地位，不是消费，也不是欲望的不断满足。

真正的幸福，是一个人能够真实地活成自己，并与他人、世界以及自己的内心建立健康关系时，所产生的长期生命状态。
