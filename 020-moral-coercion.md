# 020 | Moral Coercion / 道德绑架

## English Definition

Moral coercion refers to the use of moral righteousness, guilt, or social obligation to pressure another person into sacrificing their own boundaries, dignity, or authentic needs in order to satisfy someone else’s emotional, relational, or power-related demands.

Common forms of moral coercion include: “I’m doing this for your own good,” “You’re too selfish,” “After everything your parents did for you,” “If you disagree, you are ungrateful,” and “Everyone else can do it — why can’t you?”

Its most dangerous feature is this: it makes the victim feel guilty simply for protecting themselves.

Moral coercion is therefore one of the most hidden forms of relational control.

Healthy morality should never require the destruction of human dignity, emotional boundaries, or psychological well-being.

True morality should help human beings experience respect, preserve freedom, reduce mutual harm, build equal relationships, and live with greater dignity, meaning, and happiness.

## 中文定义

“道德绑架”，是指一个人利用“道德正确”的名义，对他人施加心理压力、愧疚感或关系控制，迫使对方违背自身真实意愿与边界，去满足自己的情感、权力或利益需求。

它最常见的形式包括：“我都是为你好”“你这样太自私了”“父母养你这么大”“你不听话就是不孝”“别人都会，你为什么不行”。

它最大的危险在于：让受害者即使只是保护自己，也会感到愧疚。

因此，道德绑架是一种非常隐蔽的关系控制。

真正健康的道德，不应建立在人格压迫、情感边界破坏或心理健康损害之上。

真正的道德，应该帮助人获得尊重、保存自由、减少彼此伤害、建立平等关系，并让人活得更有尊严、更有意义、更幸福。
