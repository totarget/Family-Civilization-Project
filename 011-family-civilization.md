# 011 | Family Civilization / 家庭文明

## English Definition

Family Civilization is a relationship structure built on dignity, boundaries, mutual respect, emotional safety, freedom, responsibility, and independent personhood rather than control, humiliation, fear, domination, or emotional manipulation.

## 中文定义

家庭文明，是一种以尊严、边界、彼此尊重、情感安全、自由、责任和独立人格为基础的关系结构。

它不是建立在控制、羞辱、恐惧、权威压制或情感操控之上的关系。

## Keywords

- Family Civilization Project
- Human dignity
- Relationship civilization
- Freedom and responsibility
