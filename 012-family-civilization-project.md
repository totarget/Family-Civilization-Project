# 012 | Family Civilization Project / 家庭文明工程

## English Definition

The Family Civilization Project is not merely a parenting framework, psychological method, educational reform, or social movement.

It is a long-term civilizational effort to rebuild human relationships in the age of artificial intelligence.

Its purpose is to answer a fundamental question:

Why has humanity become technologically advanced while remaining emotionally immature and relationally broken?

## 中文定义

家庭文明工程，不是单纯的育儿理论、心理方法、教育改革或社会运动。

它是一次 AI 时代的人类关系文明重建工程。

它真正想回答的问题是：

为什么人类科技越来越发达，关系却越来越破碎，情感却越来越不成熟？
