## English Definition

The Love-Harm Mechanism refers to the relational process through which love, when separated from personhood, boundaries, respect, emotional maturity, and the capacity to love, becomes a source of psychological harm.

Love itself does not harm people.

What harms people is love mixed with:

- control;
- possession;
- fear of loss;
- emotional dependency;
- boundary violation;
- moral blackmail;
- humiliation;
- unresolved trauma;
- immature personhood;
- the refusal to recognize the other as an independent human being.

The deepest wounds often come from those we love most because love requires vulnerability.

When a person loves deeply, they lower their defenses and offer trust, dependence, expectation, and emotional openness.

If the other person lacks the maturity to receive that vulnerability with respect, love may become the sharpest instrument of injury.

In family civilization, the Love-Harm Mechanism explains why many parents hurt children in the name of love, why partners destroy one another while claiming to care, and why intimate relationships can become sites of the deepest psychological wounds.

The task of family civilization is not to weaken love, but to civilize love.

This means transforming love from control into respect, from possession into freedom, from emotional domination into mutual dignity, and from unconscious harm into mature relational responsibility.

## Core Principle / 核心原则

> Love does not become civilized merely because it is sincere.  
> Love becomes civilized only when it learns to respect the freedom, dignity, boundaries, and personhood of the other.

> 爱不会因为真诚就自动文明。  
> 爱只有学会尊重对方的自由、尊严、边界和人格，才真正进入文明。

---

## Key Distinction / 关键区分

### Love / 爱

Love protects the other person’s dignity, freedom, boundaries, and right to become themselves.

爱，是保护对方的尊严、自由、边界，以及成为自己的权利。

### Love-Harm / 爱的伤害

Love-harm occurs when one person uses love as justification for control, possession, intrusion, emotional domination, or psychological pressure.

爱的伤害，发生在一个人用爱作为理由，去控制、占有、侵入、情感支配或心理压迫另一个人时。

---

## Mechanism / 机制拆解

The Love-Harm Mechanism usually unfolds through the following process:

1. **Vulnerability**: Love causes a person to lower their defenses.
2. **Expectation**: The loved person becomes emotionally important and trusted.
3. **Power**: The loved person gains the capacity to deeply affect the other’s inner world.
4. **Immaturity**: If the loved person lacks boundaries, emotional maturity, and respect for personhood, they misuse this relational power.
5. **Harm**: Control, humiliation, denial, manipulation, or possession enter the relationship under the name of love.
6. **Internalization**: The wounded person begins to doubt their own worth, lovability, and right to exist as themselves.
7. **Civilizational Repair**: The mechanism can be interrupted only when love is rebuilt upon dignity, boundaries, freedom, and equal personhood.

爱的伤害机制通常按照以下过程展开：

1. **脆弱打开**：爱让一个人卸下防御。
2. **期待形成**：被爱之人成为情感上重要、值得信任的人。
3. **关系权力产生**：被爱之人获得了深刻影响对方内心世界的能力。
4. **人格不成熟介入**：如果这个人缺乏边界、情绪成熟和对人格的尊重，就会误用这种关系权力。
5. **伤害发生**：控制、羞辱、否定、操纵、占有，以爱的名义进入关系。
6. **内化为自我怀疑**：受伤者开始怀疑自己的价值、是否值得被爱，以及是否有权成为自己。
7. **文明修复**：只有当爱重新建立在尊严、边界、自由和平等人格之上，这一机制才可能被打断。
