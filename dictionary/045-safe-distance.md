# 045. Safe Distance / 安全距离

## 定义 / Definition

**安全距离**，安全距离，是指在关系仍可能引发控制、争吵、羞辱、崩溃或旧伤复发时，为保护人格尊严、情绪稳定和关系可能性而设立的距离。

**Safe Distance**: Safe distance refers to the distance established to protect dignity, emotional stability, and the possibility of future relationship when closeness may still trigger control, conflict, humiliation, collapse, or old wounds.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

安全距离不是关系失败的证明，而是关系尚未具备安全条件时的文明安排。没有安全距离，强行靠近可能不是修复，而是再次伤害。

Safe distance is not proof that a relationship has failed. It is a civilized arrangement when the conditions for safety have not yet been established. Without safe distance, forced closeness may not repair the relationship; it may wound again.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明不把亲密绝对化。真正文明的关系，既允许靠近，也允许距离；既尊重爱，也尊重安全。
