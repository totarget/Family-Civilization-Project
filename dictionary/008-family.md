# 008 | Family / 家庭

## English Definition

Family should not be the smallest unit of control.

A real family is the first place where a human being learns safety, love, trust, boundaries, respect, emotional security, and the meaning of being treated as a person.

## 中文定义

家庭，不应该是最小的控制单位。

真正的家庭，应该是一个人第一次学习安全感、爱、信任、边界、尊重、情感支持，以及“被当成人”对待的地方。
