# 文明工程 / Civilization Engineering

## 中文定义

文明工程，是指将文明原则转化为可执行、可训练、可反馈、可复制、可持续优化的行动系统。它强调文明不应只停留在理念、口号、道德要求或理论书写中，而应进入日常生活、关系实践、家庭沟通和社会协作。

在家庭文明工程中，“工程”二字意味着：家庭关系可以被观察，伤害机制可以被识别，沟通方式可以被训练，关系修复可以被设计，行动效果可以被反馈，文明习惯可以被长期建立。

文明工程不是把人当作机器，而是用工程化的方法保护人的尊严、自由、爱与幸福。它的目标不是控制人，而是降低文明实践的难度，让普通人也能在具体行动中逐渐获得更文明的关系能力。

## English Definition

Civilization Engineering refers to transforming civilizational principles into action systems that are executable, trainable, reviewable, replicable, and continuously improvable. It emphasizes that civilization should not remain only in ideas, slogans, moral demands, or theoretical writing, but should enter daily life, relational practice, family communication, and social cooperation.

Within the Family Civilization Project, the word “engineering” means that family relationships can be observed, mechanisms of harm can be identified, communication patterns can be trained, relational repair can be designed, action results can be reviewed, and civilized habits can be built over time.

Civilization Engineering does not treat people as machines. It uses engineering methods to protect human dignity, freedom, love, and happiness. Its goal is not to control people, but to reduce the difficulty of civilizational practice so that ordinary people can gradually acquire more civilized relational capacities through concrete action.
