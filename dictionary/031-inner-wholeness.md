# 031. Inner Wholeness / 内在圆满

## 定义 / Definition

**内在圆满**，是指一个人的内心世界不再以他人的爱、认可、占有、陪伴或服从作为唯一支撑，而逐渐形成稳定的自我价值、情绪安放、生命意义、自我爱和行动能力。

**Inner wholeness** refers to a state in which a person’s inner world is no longer supported solely by others’ love, approval, possession, companionship, or obedience, but gradually rests on stable self-worth, emotional holding, life meaning, self-love, and the capacity for action.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

内在圆满不是不需要别人，也不是拒绝关系。它意味着一个人可以深情地进入关系，却不把自己的生命主权完全交给关系；可以珍惜被爱，却不把被爱当成存在证明；可以面对离别，却不因此失去自己。

Inner wholeness does not mean not needing others or rejecting relationships. It means being able to enter relationships deeply without handing over the sovereignty of life completely; cherishing love without treating being loved as proof of existence; facing separation without losing oneself.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明的目标之一，是让孩子在足够的爱、尊重、安全和看见中长出内在圆满。一个内在丰盛的孩子，长大后更可能建立正常的亲密关系，而不是把伴侣、孩子或他人当成填补缺口的工具。

One goal of family civilization is to help children grow inner wholeness through enough love, respect, safety, and recognition. A child who grows with inner abundance is more likely to form healthy intimate relationships as an adult, rather than using partners, children, or others as tools to fill inner deficits.
