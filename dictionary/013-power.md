# 013 | Power / 权力

## English Definition

Any power that cannot be questioned, limited, or challenged will eventually tend toward corruption.

This includes political power, religious authority, collective morality, technological systems, algorithms, corporations, social movements, family authority, and artificial intelligence.

Civilization survives only when power remains constrained.

## 中文定义

任何无法被质疑、被限制、被挑战的权力，最终都会倾向腐化。

这包括政治权力、宗教权威、集体道德、技术系统、算法、资本、社会运动、家庭权威以及人工智能。

文明能够延续，只存在于权力始终受到约束的地方。
