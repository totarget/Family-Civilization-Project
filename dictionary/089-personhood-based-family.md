# 089. Personhood-Based Family / 人格型家庭

## English

**Personhood-Based Family** refers to a family relationship structure in which every family member is first recognized as a human being before being defined by any role, identity, function, or obligation.

In a personhood-based family, a child is not merely a child who must obey. A parent is not merely an authority figure. A spouse is not someone’s possession. An elder is not merely a symbol of unquestionable authority. A provider is not the owner of the people they support.

The core principle of a personhood-based family is:

**Human beings are ends, not means.**

This means that every family member has dignity, boundaries, feelings, voice, and inner life. Family roles still exist, but they must serve human flourishing rather than erase personhood.

A personhood-based family does not mean a family without order, responsibility, or generational respect. It means that order must protect people, responsibility must not become domination, and respect must be mutual rather than one-directional.

Its main features include:

- dignity before role;
- boundaries before control;
- dialogue before command;
- responsibility before privilege;
- rules that protect everyone;
- love expressed through respect and action;
- parental authority transformed into protective authority;
- filial responsibility transformed into love with boundaries;
- marriage transformed into partnership between two independent persons.

A personhood-based family is the civilizational alternative to hierarchical family culture. It does not destroy family ethics; it renews them. It preserves care, responsibility, gratitude, and intergenerational connection while removing domination, moral coercion, and personality oppression.

In the Family Civilization Project, the personhood-based family is one of the central goals of family renewal. It is the family form in which love no longer cancels freedom, responsibility no longer cancels dignity, and family order no longer cancels the human being.

## 中文

**人格型家庭**，是指一种家庭关系结构：每一个家庭成员在被定义为父母、孩子、伴侣、长辈、晚辈、供养者或被照顾者之前，首先被承认为一个完整的人。

在人格型家庭中，孩子不只是必须服从的孩子；父母不只是天然正确的权威；伴侣不是任何人的所有物；长辈不是不可质疑的身份象征；供养者也不是被供养者的主人。

人格型家庭的核心原则是：

**人是目的，不是手段。**

这意味着，每一个家庭成员都拥有尊严、边界、感受、声音和内在世界。家庭角色依然存在，但角色必须服务于人的成长，而不能吞没人的主体性。

人格型家庭不是没有秩序、没有责任、没有长幼尊重的家庭。它强调的是：秩序必须保护人，责任不能变成支配，尊重不能只是单向要求，而应该是双向的人格承认。

人格型家庭的主要特征包括：

- 尊严先于角色；
- 边界先于控制；
- 对话先于命令；
- 责任先于特权；
- 规则保护所有人；
- 爱通过尊重和行动表达；
- 父母权威转化为守护型权威；
- 孝的责任转化为有边界的爱；
- 婚姻转化为两个独立人格之间的合作关系。

人格型家庭是等级型家庭文化的文明替代方案。它不是摧毁家庭伦理，而是更新家庭伦理。它保留照顾、责任、感恩、互助和代际承接，同时去除支配、道德绑架和人格压迫。

在家庭文明工程中，人格型家庭是家庭关系重建的核心目标之一。它意味着：爱不再取消自由，责任不再取消尊严，家庭秩序不再取消人的存在。
