# 014 | Artificial Intelligence / 人工智能

## English Definition

Artificial intelligence should not become a system for manipulating, replacing, optimizing, or controlling humanity.

The highest role of AI is to help human beings reduce suffering, preserve dignity, deepen understanding, repair relationships, and protect civilization across generations.

## 中文定义

人工智能，不应该成为操控、替代、优化或控制人类的系统。

AI 真正高级的使命，应该是帮助人类减少痛苦、守护尊严、深化理解、修复关系，并跨代保护文明。

## Keywords

- Family Civilization Project
- Human dignity
- Relationship civilization
- Freedom and responsibility
