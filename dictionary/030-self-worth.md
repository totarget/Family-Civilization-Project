# 030. Self-Worth / 自我价值

## 定义 / Definition

**自我价值**，是指一个人对自身存在价值的稳定确认。它意味着一个人不再把是否被父母认可、伴侣选择、孩子依赖、社会赞美、外部成功，作为自己是否值得存在、值得被爱、值得被尊重的唯一证明。

**Self-worth** refers to a stable recognition of the value of one’s own existence. It means that a person no longer treats parental approval, romantic selection, a child’s dependence, social praise, or external success as the sole proof that they deserve to exist, be loved, or be respected.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

自我价值不等于自大。自大常常是脆弱价值感的防御；稳定的自我价值则是一种安静的内在确认：即使失败、被拒绝、被误解、被离开，一个人的基本尊严依然存在。

Self-worth is not arrogance. Arrogance is often a defense against fragile worth. Stable self-worth is a quiet inner recognition: even after failure, rejection, misunderstanding, or abandonment, one’s basic dignity remains.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

父母给予孩子最重要的礼物之一，是帮助孩子形成稳定的自我价值。一个被无条件尊重过的孩子，长大后不必用讨好、成功、牺牲或被爱来证明自己是一个值得存在的人。

One of the most important gifts parents can give a child is stable self-worth. A child who has been unconditionally respected does not have to prove worthiness through pleasing, success, sacrifice, or being loved.
