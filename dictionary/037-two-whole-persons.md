# 037. Two Whole Persons / 两个完整的人

## 定义 / Definition

**两个完整的人**，是指健康关系中的双方都具有相对稳定的自我价值、边界、生命主权、情绪责任和自我安放能力。他们进入关系不是为了互相填补缺口，而是在各自完整的基础上彼此靠近、彼此成就、共同创造更大的生命世界。

**Two whole persons** refers to a healthy relationship in which both people possess relatively stable self-worth, boundaries, sovereignty of life, emotional responsibility, and the capacity to hold themselves. They enter the relationship not to fill each other’s deficits, but to meet, support, and create a larger world together from the foundation of inner wholeness.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

两个完整的人并不意味着两个人没有脆弱、没有需要、没有依恋。它意味着需要不变成吞噬，脆弱不变成控制，依恋不变成失去自我，亲密不变成寄生。

Two whole persons does not mean that neither person has vulnerability, need, or attachment. It means that need does not become devouring, vulnerability does not become control, attachment does not become self-loss, and intimacy does not become parasitism.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明的目标不是让孩子一生依附父母，也不是让成年人把伴侣当作父母替代品，而是让每个人逐渐成为完整的人。这样的关系才可能从控制、索取和补偿，走向自由、成全和共同成长。

The goal of family civilization is not to make children remain dependent on parents for life, nor to make adults treat partners as parental substitutes. It is to help every person gradually become whole. Only such relationships can move from control, demand, and compensation toward freedom, fulfillment, and mutual growth.
