# 052. De-triangulating the Child / 让孩子退出三角关系

## 定义 / Definition

**让孩子退出三角关系**，让孩子退出三角关系，是指在父母冲突、夫妻矛盾或家庭权力纠缠中，主动停止把孩子放在父母之间，让孩子不再充当传话人、裁判、同盟、情绪容器或替代伴侣。

**De-triangulating the Child**: De-triangulating the child means actively removing the child from parental conflict, marital tension, or family power entanglement so that the child no longer serves as messenger, judge, ally, emotional container, or substitute partner.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

孩子进入父母冲突的三角关系后，往往会失去内在安全感：爱一个父母似乎意味着背叛另一个父母；表达真实感受可能引发更大冲突；保持沉默又会承受孤独和压力。让孩子退出三角关系，是停止夫妻伤口转嫁的重要行动。

Once a child is placed in the triangle of parental conflict, inner security is often damaged: loving one parent may feel like betraying the other; expressing true feelings may trigger more conflict; remaining silent creates loneliness and pressure. De-triangulating the child is a key action in stopping spousal wound transfer.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明认为，成年人必须把成人问题带回成人之间解决。孩子有权从父母的战争中退出，重新回到孩子的位置。
