# 040. Stopping Harm / 停止伤害

## 定义 / Definition

**停止伤害**，停止伤害，是指在任何关系修复之前，首先终止正在发生或反复发生的控制、羞辱、暴力、冷暴力、情绪勒索、边界侵犯、道德绑架和人格否定等伤害性行为。

**Stopping Harm**: Stopping harm means ending ongoing or recurring behaviors of control, humiliation, violence, cold violence, emotional blackmail, boundary invasion, moral coercion, and denial of personhood before any deeper repair is attempted.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

停止伤害是关系修复的第一条件。没有停止伤害，所有道歉、沟通、和解、亲情呼唤都可能变成新的伤害。修复不能建立在继续受伤之上，亲密不能建立在持续侵犯之上。

Stopping harm is the first condition of relational repair. Without it, apologies, communication, reconciliation, and appeals to family affection may become new forms of harm. Repair cannot be built upon continued injury, and intimacy cannot be built upon ongoing violation.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明不以“家人之间没有隔夜仇”为原则，而以“任何人都不应在家庭中持续被伤害”为原则。停止伤害使关系从权力逻辑进入文明逻辑。
