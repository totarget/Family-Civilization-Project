# 从被爱到会爱 / From Being Loved to Knowing How to Love

## 中文定义

从被爱到会爱，是指人在生命早期首先需要被稳定地爱、看见和保护，由此形成内在安全感和自我价值；进入成年后，则需要在此基础上发展出真正爱他人的能力。

被爱是儿童的基本需要，会爱是成人的重要能力。没有被好好爱过的人，往往更容易把爱变成索取、控制、讨好、牺牲或投射。家庭文明的任务，既是让孩子在足够的爱中长大，也是帮助受伤的成年人重新学习爱。

从被爱到会爱，标志着关系文明的成熟。一个人不再只问“谁来爱我”，而开始学习“我如何以不伤害、不控制、不吞噬的方式去爱”。

## English Definition

From Being Loved to Knowing How to Love means that in early life a person first needs to be steadily loved, seen, and protected, thereby forming inner security and self-worth. In adulthood, one must develop the capacity to truly love others on that foundation.

Being loved is a basic need of childhood; knowing how to love is an important capacity of adulthood. Those who were not well loved are more likely to turn love into demand, control, pleasing, sacrifice, or projection. The task of Family Civilization is both to allow children to grow up in sufficient love and to help wounded adults relearn how to love.

This concept marks the maturity of relational civilization. A person no longer only asks, “Who will love me?” but begins to learn, “How can I love without harming, controlling, or consuming another person?”
