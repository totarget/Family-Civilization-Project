# 087 Emotional Safety / 情绪安全

## Chinese / 中文

**情绪安全**，是指一个人在关系中能够表达真实感受、暴露脆弱、提出需求、承认错误和呈现自我，而不必持续恐惧羞辱、惩罚、抛弃、嘲笑、攻击或报复的心理安全状态。

情绪安全是健康家庭、亲密关系和人格成长的基础。一个孩子如果在家庭中拥有情绪安全，就会逐渐形成这样的内在经验：我的感受可以被看见，我的痛苦可以被理解，我犯错以后不会被摧毁，我表达不同意见不会失去爱，我作为一个人是被尊重的。

情绪安全不是纵容情绪，也不是取消规则。它不是让孩子想怎样就怎样，而是在有边界、有责任的关系中，让孩子知道自己的情绪不会成为被羞辱、被惩罚、被抛弃的理由。

缺乏情绪安全的家庭，常常充满威胁、冷暴力、羞辱、否定、嘲讽、情绪勒索和不可预测的爆发。孩子在这样的环境中可能学会压抑、讨好、撒谎、逃避、麻木、过度警觉，甚至把亲密关系理解为危险来源。

在家庭文明视角中，情绪安全是“把孩子当成一个人”的具体表现。一个孩子不仅需要吃饱穿暖，也需要被稳定地看见、被尊重地倾听、被允许有情绪、被保护不受羞辱。

情绪安全的建立，需要父母或伴侣具备情绪自我调节能力、倾听能力、边界意识和尊重意识。文明家庭不追求没有冲突，而追求冲突中不摧毁人格；不追求永远温和，而追求在愤怒中仍然不把对方当成工具、敌人或出气筒。

情绪安全，是一个人发展自尊、自信、责任、创造力和爱人能力的重要土壤。

## English

**Emotional Safety** refers to a psychological state in which a person can express real feelings, reveal vulnerability, state needs, admit mistakes, and present the self within a relationship without constant fear of humiliation, punishment, abandonment, ridicule, attack, or retaliation.

Emotional safety is a foundation of healthy family life, intimate relationships, and personality development. When a child experiences emotional safety at home, the child gradually develops an inner experience that feelings can be seen, pain can be understood, mistakes do not destroy the self, disagreement does not result in loss of love, and one is respected as a human being.

Emotional safety is not emotional indulgence, nor does it eliminate rules. It does not mean that a child can do whatever they want. It means that within a relationship of boundaries and responsibility, the child knows that emotions will not become a reason for humiliation, punishment, or abandonment.

Families lacking emotional safety are often marked by threats, cold violence, humiliation, negation, mockery, emotional blackmail, and unpredictable explosions. Children in such environments may learn suppression, people-pleasing, lying, avoidance, numbness, hypervigilance, or the belief that intimacy is dangerous.

From the perspective of family civilization, emotional safety is a concrete expression of treating the child as a person. A child needs not only food and shelter, but also stable recognition, respectful listening, permission to have emotions, and protection from humiliation.

Building emotional safety requires emotional self-regulation, listening capacity, boundary awareness, and respect. A civilized family does not seek the absence of conflict, but seeks not to destroy personhood during conflict. It does not require perpetual gentleness, but requires that even in anger, the other person is not treated as a tool, enemy, or emotional outlet.

Emotional safety is essential soil for self-esteem, confidence, responsibility, creativity, and the ability to love.
