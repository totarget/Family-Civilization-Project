# 026. Self-Reconciliation / 自我和解

## 定义 / Definition

**自我和解**，是指一个人与自己的过去、伤口、遗憾、羞耻、愤怒、脆弱和未完成的人生部分重新建立关系，不再让生命长期被受害感、怨恨、羞耻或童年创伤所囚禁。

自我和解不是否认伤害，不是替伤害者辩护，也不是强迫自己立刻原谅一切。它是一个人把生命主权从过去收回来的过程。

**Self-reconciliation** refers to rebuilding one’s relationship with the past, wounds, regrets, shame, anger, vulnerability, and unfinished parts of life, so that life is no longer imprisoned by victimhood, resentment, shame, or childhood trauma.

Self-reconciliation is not the denial of harm, not a defense of the one who caused harm, and not a forced forgiveness of everything. It is the process of taking back the sovereignty of life from the past.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

自我和解包括三个层面：

第一，承认伤害真实存在。  
第二，承认过去无法被重新改写。  
第三，选择不再让过去继续支配未来。

Self-reconciliation contains three layers:

First, acknowledging that the harm truly happened.  
Second, acknowledging that the past cannot be rewritten.  
Third, choosing not to let the past continue to govern the future.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明不是停留在揭露伤害，而是帮助人从伤害中重新获得行动能力。自我和解使受伤者不再把一生停留在“为什么他们这样对我”的问题里，而逐渐走向“我现在如何重新成为自己”的行动中。

Family civilization does not stop at exposing harm; it helps people regain the capacity for action after harm. Self-reconciliation allows the wounded person to move beyond the question “Why did they treat me this way?” and enter the action of “How can I become myself again now?”
