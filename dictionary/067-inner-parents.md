# 067. 内在父母 / Inner Parents

## 中文定义

**内在父母**，是指成年人在内心重新发展出的稳定、温柔、有边界、有保护力的自我养育能力。它安抚受伤的内在孩子，修正内化父母留下的羞辱语言，保护自我边界，并阻止缺爱伤口继续投射到伴侣和孩子身上。

在家庭文明工程中，这一概念不是被机械照搬的心理学术语，而是被重新放入家庭、关系、人格、边界、爱、创伤修复与文明建设的语境中。它用于解释：原生家庭经验如何进入人的内在结构，如何影响成年后的亲密关系、亲子关系、自我评价、情绪反应与生命方向。

## English Definition

**Inner Parents** refers to the stable, gentle, bounded, and protective self-nurturing capacity an adult develops within. It soothes the wounded inner child, corrects the shaming language left by internalized parents, protects self-boundaries, and prevents love-deprivation wounds from being projected onto partners and children.

Within the Family Civilization Project, this concept is not mechanically imported as a psychological term. It is reinterpreted within the context of family, relationships, personhood, boundaries, love, trauma repair, and civilizational construction. It helps explain how family-of-origin experience enters inner structure and shapes adult intimacy, parent-child relationships, self-evaluation, emotional response, and life direction.

## 核心要点 / Core Points

- 该概念用于理解家庭经验如何进入人格内部。
- 它帮助解释成年人关系模式背后的深层心理结构。
- 它不是为了给伤害找借口，而是为了让伤害被看见、被修复、被停止传递。
- 在家庭文明工程中，心理学概念最终要服务于人格完整、关系文明和人的尊严。

- This concept helps understand how family experience enters personality.
- It explains the deeper psychological structure behind adult relational patterns.
- It is not used to excuse harm, but to make harm visible, repairable, and stoppable.
- In the Family Civilization Project, psychological concepts ultimately serve personality wholeness, relational civilization, and human dignity.
