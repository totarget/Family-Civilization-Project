# 016 | Relationship / 关系

## English Definition

A relationship is not merely biological connection, emotional attachment, social role, or mutual dependence.

A civilized relationship is a living structure in which human beings can remain connected without losing dignity, boundaries, freedom, responsibility, and selfhood.

The quality of a civilization is ultimately revealed in the quality of its relationships.

## 中文定义

关系，不只是血缘连接、情感依附、社会角色或相互依赖。

真正文明的关系，是人在彼此连接中，仍然能够保有尊严、边界、自由、责任和自我的生命结构。

一个文明的质量，最终会体现在它的关系质量之中。
