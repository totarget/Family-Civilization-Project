# 079 Parental Dependency Mechanism / 啃老机制

## Chinese / 中文

**啃老机制**，是指成年子女在经济、生活、情感或责任层面长期依赖父母，而家庭系统又通过纵容、兜底、控制或情感勒索不断维持这种依赖关系的结构性机制。

啃老并不只是个体“不争气”的问题。它往往是家庭关系长期失衡的结果：父母一方面抱怨子女不独立，另一方面又持续替子女承担后果；一方面希望子女成熟，另一方面又不真正允许子女脱离控制；一方面提供物质支持，另一方面用牺牲感、亏欠感和道德压力维持对子女的影响。

啃老机制的核心，不只是子女依赖父母，而是家庭系统共同维持了一种“不真正长大”的关系结构。

在这种结构中，父母可能通过过度保护、过度付出、替代决策、经济兜底、情感绑定等方式阻断子女成年化；子女则可能通过逃避责任、索取资源、拒绝独立、情绪威胁或道德反击继续停留在被供养位置。

啃老机制的危害在于，它同时伤害两代人。父母被长期消耗，子女失去现实能力；父母越兜底，子女越无力；子女越无力，父母越无法放手。最终，家庭不再是人格成长的空间，而变成彼此消耗的结构。

家庭文明强调，真正的亲情不是无限供养，也不是道德控制，而是帮助家庭成员走向人格独立、责任独立和生活独立。父母应逐步退出对子女人生的替代管理，成年子女也应逐步承担自己的选择、代价和生活秩序。

## English

**Parental Dependency Mechanism** refers to a structural family pattern in which adult children remain dependent on parents economically, practically, emotionally, or morally, while the family system continues to maintain this dependency through indulgence, rescue, control, or emotional coercion.

Parental dependency is not merely a matter of an individual child being incapable or irresponsible. It is often the result of long-term imbalance in family relationships. Parents may complain that their adult children are not independent, while repeatedly rescuing them from consequences. They may want their children to mature, yet refuse to release control. They may provide material support while using sacrifice, guilt, and moral pressure to preserve influence.

The core issue is not simply that children depend on parents, but that the whole family system maintains a structure in which true adulthood is delayed or obstructed.

Within this structure, parents may block adulthood through over-protection, excessive sacrifice, replacement decision-making, financial rescue, and emotional binding. Adult children may remain in dependency through avoidance of responsibility, resource extraction, refusal of independence, emotional threats, or moral retaliation.

The harm of this mechanism is that it damages both generations. Parents are depleted, while adult children lose practical capacity. The more parents rescue, the weaker the children become; the weaker the children become, the harder it is for parents to let go. The family ceases to be a space for human development and becomes a structure of mutual exhaustion.

Family civilization emphasizes that true kinship is neither endless provision nor moral control. It should support the movement toward personal independence, responsibility, and life autonomy. Parents should gradually withdraw from substitute management of their children’s lives, and adult children should gradually assume their own choices, consequences, and life order.
