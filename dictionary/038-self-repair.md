# 038. Self-Repair / 自我修复

## 定义 / Definition

**自我修复**，是指一个人在受到原生家庭、亲密关系、社会评价或人生创伤影响之后，通过觉知、哀悼、理解、行动、边界、自我父母、自我爱和关系重建，逐渐恢复人格完整、生命主权和幸福能力的过程。

**Self-repair** refers to the process through which a person, after being affected by family of origin, intimate relationships, social judgment, or life trauma, gradually restores personal wholeness, sovereignty of life, and the capacity for happiness through awareness, mourning, understanding, action, boundaries, inner parenting, self-love, and relational reconstruction.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

自我修复不是一次觉悟，而是长期实践。它既包括认知上的理解，也包括身体上的稳定、情绪上的安放、关系中的边界、生活中的行动，以及对未来的重新承诺。

Self-repair is not a single awakening but a long-term practice. It includes cognitive understanding, bodily stability, emotional holding, relational boundaries, life action, and a renewed commitment to the future.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明工程不是只揭示伤害，也不是只提出理念。它的最终任务，是帮助人把受伤后的生命重新建设出来。自我修复是从个人痛苦走向家庭文明实践的关键桥梁。

The Family Civilization Project does not merely reveal harm or propose ideas. Its ultimate task is to help people rebuild life after injury. Self-repair is the key bridge from personal suffering to the practice of family civilization.
