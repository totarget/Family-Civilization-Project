# 049. Re-earned Parental Trust / 重新赢得信任

## 定义 / Definition

**重新赢得信任**，重新赢得信任，是指父母或伤害者在关系破裂或疏远之后，不再以身份、血缘、养育恩情或道德义务要求亲近，而是通过长期稳定的尊重、边界和行动改变重新获得对方信任。

**Re-earned Parental Trust**: Re-earned trust means that parents or those who caused harm, after rupture or distance, no longer demand closeness through status, blood ties, the debt of upbringing, or moral obligation, but regain trust through long-term respect, boundaries, and changed behavior.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

信任不是亲情的自动赠品。长期被控制、羞辱、忽视或伤害之后，受伤者的距离是一种保护。重新赢得信任，需要时间、耐心、稳定和不再伤害。

Trust is not an automatic gift of family affection. After long-term control, humiliation, neglect, or harm, distance is a form of protection. Re-earning trust requires time, patience, consistency, and the cessation of harm.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明认为，父母身份不能免除信任责任。真正的亲情不是依靠血缘强迫维持，而是依靠人格可信度重新建立。
