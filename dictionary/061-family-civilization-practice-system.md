# 家庭文明实践系统 / Family Civilization Practice System

## 中文定义

家庭文明实践系统，是指围绕家庭文明原则建立的一整套行动结构，包括家庭会议、文明记录、关系复盘、AI顾问协助、亲子沟通练习、道歉与边界训练、实践反馈和长期改进。

它不同于单次咨询或单篇文章。家庭文明实践系统强调连续性和可重复性：一次家庭会议可能只是开始，真正的改变来自持续记录、持续反馈、持续调整和持续练习。

家庭文明实践系统的目标，是把“知道应该尊重孩子”“知道不能伤害家人”“知道要修复关系”等认知，转化为真实生活中可以执行的步骤。它让家庭文明从理念进入日常生活。

## English Definition

The Family Civilization Practice System refers to a complete action structure built around the principles of Family Civilization. It includes family meetings, civilized recording, relational review, AI advisor support, parent-child communication practice, apology and boundary training, practice feedback, and long-term improvement.

It differs from a single consultation or a single article. The practice system emphasizes continuity and repeatability. One family meeting may only be the beginning; real change comes from continuous recording, feedback, adjustment, and practice.

Its goal is to transform cognitive awareness—such as knowing that children should be respected, that family members should not be harmed, and that relationships should be repaired—into executable steps in real life. It allows Family Civilization to enter daily living.
