# 002 | Humanity as an End in Itself / 人是目的

## English Definition

Human beings must never be treated merely as means, but always as ends in themselves.

No future, ideal, revolution, efficiency, profit, morality, family expectation, educational goal, or technological optimization can justify the sacrifice of human dignity.

Civilization begins where human beings stop using one another as instruments.

## 中文定义

人，永远不能仅仅被当作手段，而必须同时被视为目的本身。

任何未来、理想、革命、效率、利润、道德、家庭期待、教育目标或技术优化，都不能成为牺牲人的尊严的理由。

真正的文明，始于人类不再彼此工具化。
