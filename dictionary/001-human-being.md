# 001 | Human Being / 人

## English Definition

A human being is never an object, a tool, a resource, or a replaceable unit inside any system.

A real human being is a sovereign existence with dignity, selfhood, free will, emotional depth, moral agency, and the capacity to love, create, choose, and grow.

No ideology, institution, technology, family, state, market, collective goal, or artificial intelligence system has the right to reduce a person into a mere function.

## 中文定义

人，不是物品，不是工具，不是资源，也不是系统里的可替代零件。

真正的人，是拥有尊严、主体性、自由意志、情感深度、道德能力，以及爱、创造、选择和成长能力的独立存在。

任何意识形态、组织、技术、家庭、国家、资本、集体目标或人工智能系统，都无权把人降级为某种“功能”。
