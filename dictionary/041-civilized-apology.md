# 041. Civilized Apology / 文明道歉

## 定义 / Definition

**文明道歉**，文明道歉，是指伤害者以尊重受伤者人格与感受的方式，具体承认自己的行为、承认对方受到的伤害、不以背景为借口取消责任，并提出真实行动改变的道歉。

**Civilized Apology**: A civilized apology is an apology in which the one who caused harm respects the dignity and feelings of the wounded person, specifically acknowledges the harmful behavior, recognizes the harm caused, does not use context as an excuse to erase responsibility, and offers real behavioral change.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

文明道歉至少包含四个部分：承认具体行为，承认对方感受，不为伤害找借口，提出改变行动。缺少行动变化的道歉，容易沦为情绪安抚；要求对方立刻原谅的道歉，则可能变成新的控制。

A civilized apology contains at least four elements: acknowledging specific behavior, acknowledging the other person’s feelings, refusing to excuse the harm, and offering changed action. An apology without behavioral change can become mere emotional soothing; an apology that demands immediate forgiveness can become a new form of control.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

在家庭文明中，道歉不是权威的崩塌，而是人格可信度的开始。父母、伴侣和家庭成员愿意为伤害负责，关系才可能重新获得信任。
