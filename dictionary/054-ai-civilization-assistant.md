# AI文明助理 / AI Civilization Assistant

## 中文定义

AI文明助理，是指在文明原则、伦理边界和人的尊严之下，协助个人、家庭或共同体完成沟通、记录、反思、修复、学习和行动改进的人工智能角色。

AI文明助理不同于单纯的效率工具。它不仅帮助人更快完成任务，更重要的是帮助人以更文明的方式面对自己、他人和关系。它不替代人的主体性，而是协助人恢复主体性；它不强化控制，而是帮助减少控制；它不制造依赖，而是帮助人逐步形成更成熟的判断、表达与行动能力。

在家庭文明工程中，AI文明助理是AI与人类文明协作的基础形态之一。它代表一种方向：AI不只是生产力工具，也可以成为关系文明、人格成长和家庭修复的协作者。

## English Definition

The AI Civilization Assistant refers to an artificial intelligence role that supports individuals, families, or communities in communication, documentation, reflection, repair, learning, and practical improvement under civilizational principles, ethical boundaries, and human dignity.

It differs from a mere productivity tool. Its purpose is not only to help people complete tasks faster, but to help people face themselves, others, and relationships in a more civilized way. It does not replace human agency; it helps restore it. It does not strengthen control; it helps reduce control. It does not create dependence; it helps people gradually develop more mature judgment, expression, and action.

Within the Family Civilization Project, the AI Civilization Assistant is one foundational form of AI-human civilizational collaboration. It represents the direction that AI can become not only a productivity tool, but a collaborator in relational civilization, personal growth, and family repair.
