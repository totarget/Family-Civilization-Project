# 084 For-Your-Own-Good Control / 为你好式控制

## Chinese / 中文

**为你好式控制**，是指父母或亲密关系中的一方以“我是为你好”为名，将自己的意志、焦虑、判断和期待强加给他人，并以爱、牺牲、经验或权威为理由，否定对方的感受、选择、边界和主体性。

“为你好”本身可以是真诚关心，也可以变成控制的道德包装。判断二者的关键，不在于说话者是否自称有爱，而在于对方是否仍然被尊重为一个独立的人。

当“为你好”意味着提供建议、表达担心、尊重选择，它可以是爱。当“为你好”意味着命令、逼迫、羞辱、剥夺选择、否定感受、制造亏欠感，它就成为控制。

为你好式控制的常见逻辑是：因为我爱你，所以你必须听我的；因为我经验更多，所以你的感受不重要；因为我为你付出，所以你没有拒绝的资格；因为我是父母，所以我可以替你决定人生。

这种控制最危险之处在于，它披着爱的外衣，使被控制者难以反抗。一旦反抗，就会被指责为不懂事、不孝、没良心、不知好歹。于是，控制不再表现为赤裸暴力，而表现为道德化的爱。

在家庭文明视角中，真正的爱必须尊重人的主体性。爱可以建议，但不能替代选择；爱可以保护，但不能取消边界；爱可以表达担心，但不能把对方变成自己的工具。

“为你好”的文明边界是：建议可以提出，选择必须归还；关心可以表达，人生不能代替；爱不能成为剥夺自由的理由。

## English

**For-Your-Own-Good Control** refers to a pattern in which a parent or intimate partner imposes their will, anxiety, judgment, and expectations on another person under the claim of “I am doing this for your own good,” using love, sacrifice, experience, or authority to deny the other person’s feelings, choices, boundaries, and subjectivity.

“For your own good” can be sincere care, but it can also become a moral wrapper for control. The difference does not lie in whether the speaker claims love, but whether the other person is still respected as an independent human being.

When “for your own good” means offering advice, expressing concern, and respecting choice, it may be love. When it means commanding, coercing, humiliating, removing choice, denying feelings, or creating guilt, it becomes control.

The common logic is: because I love you, you must listen to me; because I have more experience, your feelings do not matter; because I have sacrificed for you, you have no right to refuse; because I am your parent, I may decide your life.

This form of control is dangerous because it wears the clothing of love. The controlled person finds it difficult to resist, because resistance is then labeled immature, unfilial, heartless, or ungrateful. Control no longer appears as naked violence, but as moralized love.

From the perspective of family civilization, true love must respect human subjectivity. Love may advise, but cannot replace choice; love may protect, but cannot erase boundaries; love may express concern, but cannot turn another person into a tool.

The civilized boundary of “for your own good” is this: advice may be offered, but choice must be returned; concern may be expressed, but life cannot be replaced; love cannot become a reason to deprive freedom.
