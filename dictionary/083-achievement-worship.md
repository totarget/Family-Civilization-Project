# 083 Achievement Worship / 成绩崇拜

## Chinese / 中文

**成绩崇拜**，是指家庭、学校或社会将考试成绩、排名、升学结果和外部成功视为衡量一个孩子价值的最高标准，甚至以此替代对人格、幸福、关系、创造力和生命完整性的关注。

成绩本身并不是问题。学习、训练、努力和结果都很重要。问题在于，当成绩被绝对化、神圣化、人格化，孩子就不再被视为一个完整的人，而被简化为分数、排名和升学路径。

成绩崇拜的典型表现包括：只关注分数，不关心孩子是否快乐；只看排名，不看孩子的内在状态；把孩子的成绩当作父母面子的来源；把升学结果等同于人生价值；把失败、退步或普通视为耻辱。

成绩崇拜会制造一种危险的家庭逻辑：孩子成绩好，才值得被肯定；孩子表现优秀，才配得到温和对待；孩子考得不好，就可以被羞辱、责骂、打击。这样，爱变成了条件，尊严变成了奖励，人格被成绩绑架。

在家庭文明视角中，成绩不能凌驾于人之上。成绩是成长的一部分，不是人的全部。教育应当帮助孩子发展学习能力、责任能力、思考能力、关系能力、创造能力和幸福能力，而不是把孩子压缩成一台考试机器。

成绩崇拜的反面不是反智、反学习或纵容懒惰，而是让学习重新服务于人的成长。真正文明的教育，应当承认：一个孩子首先是人，然后才是学生；人格完整比排名更根本，生命幸福比单一成功更重要。

## English

**Achievement Worship** refers to a family, school, or social pattern in which test scores, rankings, admissions, and external success are treated as the highest measure of a child’s value, often replacing concern for personality, happiness, relationships, creativity, and the wholeness of life.

Achievement itself is not the problem. Learning, effort, discipline, and results all matter. The problem begins when achievement becomes absolute, sacred, and person-defining. The child is no longer seen as a whole human being, but reduced to scores, rankings, and academic pathways.

Typical signs include caring only about scores, ignoring the child’s emotional life, treating achievement as parental face, equating admissions with life value, and treating failure, regression, or ordinariness as shame.

Achievement worship creates a dangerous family logic: a child deserves affirmation only when scores are good; a child deserves gentleness only when performance is excellent; a child who performs poorly may be humiliated, scolded, or attacked. Love becomes conditional, dignity becomes a reward, and personality becomes held hostage by achievement.

From the perspective of family civilization, achievement must not stand above the human being. Achievement is part of growth, not the whole of a person. Education should develop learning capacity, responsibility, thinking, relationships, creativity, and the capacity for happiness, rather than compressing the child into an examination machine.

The opposite of achievement worship is not anti-intellectualism, anti-learning, or indulgence of laziness. It is the restoration of learning as a servant of human development. A civilized education recognizes that a child is first a person and then a student; personality integrity is more fundamental than ranking, and life happiness is more important than a single form of success.
