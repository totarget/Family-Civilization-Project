# 081 Deprivation of Responsibility / 责任剥夺

## Chinese / 中文

**责任剥夺**，是指家庭或教育系统以保护、替代、控制、包办、兜底等方式，剥夺一个人在成长过程中学习选择、承担后果、修正错误和发展责任能力的机会。

责任并不是成年之后突然出现的能力，而是在成长过程中通过一次次选择、尝试、失败、承担和修正逐步形成的人格功能。如果一个孩子长期没有机会为自己的行为承担适度后果，他就很难发展出真正的责任意识。

责任剥夺常常发生在两种家庭模式中。第一种是溺爱型家庭，父母替孩子处理所有困难、承担所有后果，使孩子无法理解行为与结果之间的关系。第二种是控制型家庭，父母替孩子做所有决定，使孩子虽然服从，却没有真正练习选择与负责。

责任剥夺的结果，是孩子可能在成年后出现两种相反但本质相同的状态：一种是依赖与逃避，遇到问题等待他人解决；另一种是反抗与混乱，拒绝规则却也缺乏自我管理能力。

责任剥夺并不是“爱孩子”，而是剥夺孩子成为责任主体的机会。一个人如果从未被允许选择，就难以真正学会负责；一个人如果从未承担后果，就难以真正理解自由的重量。

家庭文明强调，自由与责任必须同时成长。尊重孩子不是放任孩子，培养责任也不是压迫孩子。真正健康的家庭，应当在保护孩子尊严的前提下，给予他与年龄、能力相匹配的选择空间、责任空间和后果空间。

## English

**Deprivation of Responsibility** refers to the process by which a family or educational system deprives a person of opportunities to learn choice, consequence, correction, and responsibility through protection, replacement, control, over-management, or repeated rescue.

Responsibility is not a capacity that suddenly appears in adulthood. It develops gradually through repeated experiences of choosing, trying, failing, bearing consequences, and correcting mistakes. If a child has little opportunity to experience appropriate consequences, genuine responsibility cannot mature.

This deprivation often occurs in two family patterns. In indulgent families, parents solve every difficulty and bear every consequence, preventing the child from understanding the relationship between action and outcome. In controlling families, parents make every decision for the child, producing obedience without real practice in choice and accountability.

The result may appear in two opposite but related adult patterns: dependency and avoidance, in which the person waits for others to solve problems; or rebellion and disorder, in which the person rejects rules but lacks self-management.

Depriving a child of responsibility is not love. It deprives the child of the chance to become a responsible subject. A person who has never been allowed to choose may struggle to take responsibility; a person who has never faced consequences may never understand the weight of freedom.

Family civilization emphasizes that freedom and responsibility must develop together. Respecting a child does not mean permissiveness, and cultivating responsibility does not mean oppression. A healthy family provides age-appropriate space for choice, responsibility, and consequences while protecting the child’s dignity.
