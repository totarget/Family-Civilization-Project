# HUMANS ARE ENDS

This section is the philosophical foundation of the Family Civilization Project.

It explores the principle that human beings are ends, not tools, and develops this principle through ancient philosophy, Kantian ethics, human dignity, family relationships, children’s personhood, and AI ethics.

The first Chinese batch has been prepared. A native English edition will be expanded in later updates.
