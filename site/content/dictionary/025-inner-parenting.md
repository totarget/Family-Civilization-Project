# 025. Inner Parenting / 自我父母

## 定义 / Definition

**自我父母**，是指成年人在内心逐渐发展出一种照顾自己、安抚自己、保护自己、引导自己、尊重自己的能力。

当原生家庭没有给予孩子足够的爱、看见、安全、边界和尊重时，成年人不能永远等待外部父母重新出现，而需要在内在世界中重建一种稳定、温柔、清醒、有力量的父母性功能。

**Inner parenting** refers to the adult capacity to care for, soothe, protect, guide, and respect oneself from within.

When the family of origin did not provide enough love, recognition, safety, boundaries, and respect, the adult cannot wait forever for external parents to reappear. A stable, gentle, lucid, and strong parental function must be rebuilt within the inner world.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

自我父母不是幻想一个完美父母，也不是否认真实父母曾经造成的伤害。它是一种人格修复能力：在恐惧时接住自己，在失败时不羞辱自己，在孤独时陪伴自己，在混乱时引导自己，在被侵犯时保护自己。

Inner parenting is not the fantasy of perfect parents, nor is it the denial of harm caused by real parents. It is a capacity for personal repair: holding oneself in fear, refusing self-humiliation in failure, accompanying oneself in loneliness, guiding oneself in confusion, and protecting oneself when violated.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明承认：很多人走入成年时，心里仍然住着一个受伤、哭泣、没有被好好爱过的孩子。自我父母的意义，是让这个内在孩子不再只能向伴侣、孩子、朋友或社会索取补偿，而能够逐渐在自己心中获得稳定的照顾。

Family civilization recognizes that many adults carry within them a wounded and crying child who was never properly loved. The meaning of inner parenting is to help this inner child stop seeking compensation only from partners, children, friends, or society, and gradually receive stable care from within.
