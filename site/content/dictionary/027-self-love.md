# 027. Self-Love / 自我爱

## 定义 / Definition

**自我爱**，是指一个人承认自身存在具有不可替代的价值，并以尊重、照顾、保护、诚实和成长的方式对待自己。

自我爱不是自私，不是任性，不是以自己为中心伤害他人。真正的自我爱，是人格完整的基础，也是一个人能够健康爱人的前提。

**Self-love** refers to recognizing the irreplaceable value of one’s own existence and treating oneself with respect, care, protection, honesty, and growth.

Self-love is not selfishness, willfulness, or harming others through self-centeredness. True self-love is the foundation of personal wholeness and a precondition for loving others in a healthy way.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

缺乏自我爱的人，容易通过讨好、牺牲、忍耐、迎合或被他人选择来证明自己值得被爱。拥有自我爱的人，不会把自己的尊严交给他人评判，也不会为了留住关系而放弃自我。

A person without self-love often tries to prove worthiness through pleasing, sacrifice, endurance, compliance, or being chosen by others. A person with self-love does not hand dignity over to others for judgment and does not abandon the self merely to preserve a relationship.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明认为，父母应当帮助孩子形成最早的自我爱：让孩子知道自己不是因为成绩、听话、成功或孝顺才值得被爱，而是因为他作为一个人本身就具有尊严和价值。

Family civilization holds that parents should help children form the earliest foundation of self-love: the child should know that they are not worthy of love because of grades, obedience, success, or filial compliance, but because they possess dignity and value as a human being.
