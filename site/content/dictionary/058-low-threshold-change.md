# 低门槛改变 / Low-Threshold Change

## 中文定义

低门槛改变，是指不要求一个人或一个家庭在一开始就完全理解、完全认同、完全觉醒，而是通过一个小行动、一次小尝试、一次较安全的沟通，让改变从最低阻力处开始发生。

许多家庭之所以长期停留在痛苦中，并不是因为所有成员都拒绝幸福，而是因为改变的门槛太高：承认错误太难，重新沟通太难，道歉太难，停止控制太难，打开伤口太难。因此，家庭文明工程必须提供低门槛的行动入口。

低门槛改变强调：不必等到所有道理都想明白再行动。可以先做一次家庭会议，先记录一次冲突，先少说一句羞辱的话，先给孩子一次完整表达的机会。行动一旦产生微小改善，新的认知和信任就可能从行动中长出来。

## English Definition

Low-Threshold Change means not requiring a person or family to fully understand, fully agree, or fully awaken at the beginning. Instead, change begins through one small action, one small attempt, or one relatively safe communication practice.

Many families remain in suffering not because every member rejects happiness, but because the threshold for change is too high: admitting mistakes is difficult, reopening communication is difficult, apology is difficult, stopping control is difficult, and touching wounds is difficult. Therefore, Family Civilization must provide low-threshold entrances into action.

Low-Threshold Change emphasizes that one does not have to wait until every principle is fully understood before acting. A family can first hold one meeting, record one conflict, say one fewer humiliating sentence, or give the child one full opportunity to express. Once small improvements occur through action, new understanding and trust may grow from action itself.
