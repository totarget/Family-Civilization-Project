# 036. Let the Wound Stop Here / 伤口到我为止

## 定义 / Definition

**伤口到我为止**，是指一个人在意识到自己曾经受伤之后，选择不再把伤害、恐惧、控制、羞辱、情感匮乏和未完成的痛苦继续转嫁给伴侣、孩子或下一代。

**Let the wound stop here** refers to the choice, after becoming aware of one’s own wounds, not to continue transferring harm, fear, control, shame, emotional deprivation, and unfinished pain to partners, children, or the next generation.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

它不是否认自己受过伤，也不是要求受伤者独自承担所有责任。它是成年后的文明选择：伤害曾经经过自己，但不必继续通过自己传递下去。

It does not deny that one has been harmed, nor does it demand that the wounded person bear all responsibility alone. It is an adult civilizational choice: harm may have passed through the self, but it does not have to continue passing on through the self.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

这是家庭文明工程最重要的代际伦理之一。真正的家庭文明不是没有伤口，而是每一代人都努力让伤口在自己这里减少、被看见、被处理，而不是继续作为命运传给下一代。

This is one of the most important intergenerational ethics of the Family Civilization Project. True family civilization is not the absence of wounds, but each generation’s effort to reduce, see, and process wounds rather than passing them on as destiny.
