# 034. Childhood Mourning / 童年哀悼

## 定义 / Definition

**童年哀悼**，是指成年人清醒地承认自己童年中某些爱、保护、看见、尊重和安全感确实没有得到，并允许自己为这些缺失悲伤、愤怒、流泪和告别。

**Childhood mourning** refers to the adult process of lucidly acknowledging that certain forms of love, protection, recognition, respect, and safety were truly absent in childhood, and allowing oneself to grieve, feel anger, cry, and say farewell to these losses.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

童年哀悼不是沉溺过去，而是停止向错误的地方继续乞讨。它承认：有些过去无法弥补，有些父母无法成为理想父母，有些期待不会以原来的方式回来。正因为承认这一点，生命才有可能重新向前。

Childhood mourning is not indulgence in the past; it is the end of begging at the wrong door. It recognizes that some past losses cannot be repaired, some parents cannot become ideal parents, and some expectations will not return in the original form. Precisely by acknowledging this, life can move forward again.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明帮助成年人完成童年哀悼，使他们不再把未被满足的爱转嫁给伴侣、孩子或社会。哀悼之后，一个人才可能从“等待过去补偿我”转向“我现在开始重新养育自己”。

Family civilization helps adults complete childhood mourning so that unmet love is no longer transferred to partners, children, or society. After mourning, a person can move from “waiting for the past to compensate me” to “beginning to re-parent myself now.”
