# 047. Relearning Love / 重新学习爱

## 定义 / Definition

**重新学习爱**，重新学习爱，是指父母、伴侣或家庭成员在意识到原有爱的方式包含控制、伤害、焦虑、投射或占有之后，重新学习以尊重、倾听、边界、责任和行动改变来表达爱。

**Relearning Love**: Relearning love means that parents, partners, or family members, after recognizing that their former way of loving contained control, harm, anxiety, projection, or possession, learn again to express love through respect, listening, boundaries, responsibility, and changed action.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

许多人并非不爱，而是不会爱。他们把控制当爱，把牺牲当爱，把焦虑当爱，把替孩子安排人生当爱，把要求伴侣满足全部情绪当爱。重新学习爱，就是让爱重新接受文明原则的约束。

Many people do not lack love; they lack the capacity to love well. They mistake control, sacrifice, anxiety, arranging a child’s life, or demanding that a partner satisfy all emotions for love. Relearning love means placing love again under the guidance of civilized principles.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明认为，爱不是本能的充分理由。爱必须被学习、被约束、被修正，并落实为不伤害人的行动。
