# 050. Spousal Wound Transfer / 夫妻伤口转嫁

## 定义 / Definition

**夫妻伤口转嫁**，夫妻伤口转嫁，是指夫妻双方或其中一方没有处理自己的原生家庭创伤、婚姻失望、情感匮乏、愤怒与孤独，而将这些未被修复的伤口转移到孩子身上，使孩子承担不属于自己的痛苦。

**Spousal Wound Transfer**: Spousal wound transfer refers to the pattern in which one or both spouses fail to process their family-of-origin wounds, marital disappointment, emotional deprivation, anger, and loneliness, and transfer these unhealed wounds onto the child, making the child carry pain that does not belong to them.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

典型表现包括：让孩子站队，向孩子倾诉对伴侣的怨恨，把孩子变成情绪伴侣，把婚姻失败转化为对孩子的控制，把孩子的成就当作自我补偿。

Typical forms include making the child take sides, confiding resentment toward the spouse to the child, turning the child into an emotional partner, converting marital failure into control over the child, and treating the child’s success as compensation for the self.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明强调，夫妻的问题应由成年人面对，不能让孩子成为婚姻创伤的承担者。孩子不是父母冲突的同盟、裁判、治疗师或替代伴侣。
