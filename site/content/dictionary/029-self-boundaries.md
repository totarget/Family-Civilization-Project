# 029. Self-Boundaries / 自我边界

## 定义 / Definition

**自我边界**，是指一个人清楚地知道自己是谁、需要什么、不能接受什么、可以承担什么、不应承担什么，并能够在关系中保护自己的身体、情绪、时间、尊严、选择和生命方向。

**Self-boundaries** refer to a person’s clarity about who they are, what they need, what they cannot accept, what they can bear, what they should not bear, and their ability to protect their body, emotions, time, dignity, choices, and direction of life in relationships.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

没有自我边界的人，容易用讨好换取关系，用牺牲换取认可，用忍耐维持表面和平。拥有自我边界的人，能够在爱中保持自我，在亲密中保留自由，在关系中守住尊严。

A person without self-boundaries often trades pleasing for relationship, sacrifice for recognition, and endurance for superficial peace. A person with self-boundaries can remain oneself in love, preserve freedom in intimacy, and maintain dignity in relationships.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明强调，孩子从小就应当被允许拥有身体边界、情感边界和人生边界。成年后的自我边界，往往来自童年被尊重的经验；如果童年边界长期被侵犯，成年人就需要重新学习边界。

Family civilization emphasizes that children should be allowed to possess bodily, emotional, and life boundaries from early life. Adult self-boundaries often arise from childhood experiences of being respected. If childhood boundaries were repeatedly violated, boundaries must be relearned in adulthood.
