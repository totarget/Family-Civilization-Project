# 044. Limited Contact / 有限联系

## 定义 / Definition

**有限联系**，有限联系，是指在关系尚未足够安全、边界尚未被稳定尊重、旧有伤害模式仍可能重演时，受伤者通过减少频率、缩短时长、限定话题、改变沟通方式或暂时保持距离来保护自己的关系选择。

**Limited Contact**: Limited contact refers to a relational choice in which the wounded person protects themselves by reducing frequency, shortening duration, limiting topics, changing communication methods, or temporarily keeping distance when the relationship is not yet safe, boundaries are not reliably respected, or old patterns may repeat.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

有限联系不是报复，不是冷漠，也不是彻底否定亲情。它的核心不是“我不要你”，而是“在关系尚未安全之前，我需要保护自己”。

Limited contact is not revenge, coldness, or a total denial of family bonds. Its core message is not “I do not want you,” but “before this relationship becomes safe, I need to protect myself.”

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明承认，并非所有关系都适合立刻亲密。有些关系可以重新靠近，有些关系需要保持有限联系，有些关系需要暂停。尊重有限联系，是尊重人的尊严、安全和边界。
