# 051. Emotional Alliance with the Child / 把孩子拉成情绪同盟

## 定义 / Definition

**把孩子拉成情绪同盟**，把孩子拉成情绪同盟，是指父母在夫妻冲突、家庭矛盾或个人痛苦中，有意或无意地让孩子站在自己一边，承接自己的情绪，认同自己对另一方的指责，甚至承担安慰、支持和陪伴父母的角色。

**Emotional Alliance with the Child**: Emotional alliance with the child refers to the pattern in which a parent, amid marital conflict, family tension, or personal suffering, consciously or unconsciously makes the child take their side, carry their emotions, agree with their blame toward the other parent, or serve as comforter, supporter, and companion.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

这种同盟表面上似乎是亲密，实质上是角色错位。孩子被迫进入成人冲突，失去作为孩子的安全位置，也可能在内心背负对另一位父母的敌意、内疚或撕裂。

This alliance may appear intimate on the surface, but it is a role reversal. The child is forced into adult conflict, loses the safe position of being a child, and may carry hostility, guilt, or inner division toward the other parent.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明要求父母保护孩子免于被卷入成人冲突。父母可以有痛苦，但不能把孩子变成自己的情绪战友。
