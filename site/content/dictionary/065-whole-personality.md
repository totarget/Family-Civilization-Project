# 完整人格 / Whole Personality

## 中文定义

完整人格，是指一个人逐渐形成稳定的自我价值、清晰的边界、基本的情绪调节能力、承担责任的能力、爱与被爱的能力，以及不把自己的生命主权完全交给他人的内在结构。

完整人格不是完美人格。一个完整的人仍然会脆弱、会痛苦、会犯错、会需要爱，但他不会把自己的全部价值建立在别人的认可上，也不会把自己的全部幸福压在某一个关系上。

在家庭文明工程中，完整人格是一切健康关系的基础。家庭教育的目标不是培养听话的孩子，而是帮助孩子成为具有尊严、自由、责任、爱与幸福能力的人。

## English Definition

Whole Personality refers to the gradual formation of stable self-worth, clear boundaries, basic emotional regulation, the capacity to take responsibility, the capacity to love and be loved, and an inner structure that does not hand one’s sovereignty of life entirely to others.

A whole personality is not a perfect personality. A whole person can still be vulnerable, suffer, make mistakes, and need love. But such a person does not build all self-worth on others’ approval, nor place all happiness upon a single relationship.

Within the Family Civilization Project, Whole Personality is the foundation of all healthy relationships. The goal of family education is not to produce obedient children, but to help children become people with dignity, freedom, responsibility, love, and the capacity for happiness.
