# 035. Inner Child / 内在孩子

## 定义 / Definition

**内在孩子**，是指成年人内心仍然保留的童年经验、情绪记忆、未被满足的渴望、受伤的自我部分，以及仍然期待被看见、被保护、被安抚、被爱的生命状态。

**Inner child** refers to the childhood experiences, emotional memories, unmet longings, wounded parts of the self, and states of life within an adult that still long to be seen, protected, soothed, and loved.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

内在孩子不是幼稚的代名词，而是早期生命经验在成年人格中的持续存在。当旧伤被触发时，成年人可能会以童年时的恐惧、愤怒、无助或渴望反应，而不是以当下成年人的稳定能力反应。

The inner child is not a synonym for childishness. It is the continuing presence of early life experiences within adult personality. When old wounds are triggered, an adult may react from childhood fear, anger, helplessness, or longing rather than from present adult stability.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明承认许多成年人的内心仍然住着一个没有被好好爱过的孩子。修复内在孩子，不是回到软弱，而是让被遗留在过去的生命部分重新被看见、被接住、被整合。

Family civilization recognizes that many adults carry within them a child who was never properly loved. Healing the inner child is not a return to weakness; it is the process of seeing, holding, and integrating the parts of life left behind in the past.
