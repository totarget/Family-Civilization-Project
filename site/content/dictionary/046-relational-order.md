# 046. Relational Order / 关系秩序

## 定义 / Definition

**关系秩序**，关系秩序，是指一段关系中关于尊重、边界、责任、表达、距离、权利与义务的基本结构。健康的关系秩序使关系成员知道彼此如何相处、如何停止伤害、如何表达需要、如何承担责任。

**Relational Order**: Relational order refers to the basic structure of respect, boundaries, responsibility, expression, distance, rights, and obligations in a relationship. A healthy relational order helps members know how to relate, how to stop harm, how to express needs, and how to take responsibility.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

家庭关系的痛苦，很多时候不是单一事件造成的，而是旧的关系秩序长期失衡：父母高高在上，孩子只能服从；伴侣互相指责，却没有规则；亲情以爱为名，却缺少边界。关系修复的本质，是建立新的关系秩序。

Family suffering is often not caused by a single event, but by a long-standing disorder: parents standing above children, children forced into obedience, partners blaming each other without rules, and affection lacking boundaries. The essence of repair is the building of a new relational order.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明工程的目标之一，是把家庭从命令秩序、权力秩序和情绪秩序，转化为尊严秩序、边界秩序和协商秩序。
