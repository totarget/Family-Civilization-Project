# 028. Self-Soothing / 自我安抚

## 定义 / Definition

**自我安抚**，是指一个人在痛苦、恐惧、孤独、焦虑、失落、愤怒或被触发旧伤时，能够以稳定、温柔、清醒的方式照顾自己的情绪，而不是把全部情绪压力立即转交给他人承担。

**Self-soothing** refers to the ability to care for one’s emotions in a stable, gentle, and lucid way during pain, fear, loneliness, anxiety, loss, anger, or activation of old wounds, rather than immediately transferring all emotional pressure to others.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

自我安抚不是压抑情绪，也不是假装没事。它包括看见情绪、命名情绪、允许情绪存在，并通过身体、呼吸、书写、行动、秩序、陪伴、求助等方式让自己逐渐回到稳定状态。

Self-soothing is not emotional suppression or pretending that nothing happened. It includes seeing emotions, naming emotions, allowing emotions to exist, and gradually returning to stability through the body, breathing, writing, action, order, companionship, and appropriate help-seeking.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

许多成年人之所以在亲密关系中失控，是因为他们童年从未被稳定安抚过。家庭文明的任务之一，是让孩子在早期关系中获得足够的安抚经验，并帮助受伤成年人重新学习安抚自己。

Many adults lose control in intimate relationships because they were never steadily soothed in childhood. One task of family civilization is to give children sufficient early experiences of being soothed and to help wounded adults relearn self-soothing.
