# 文明记录 / Civilized Recording

## 中文定义

文明记录，是指在家庭沟通、冲突复盘、关系修复和成长实践中，以尊重事实、尊重感受、尊重边界的方式记录发生过的事情，而不是以攻击、控诉、审判或控制为目的记录他人。

文明记录的核心不是留下证据来惩罚谁，而是帮助家庭成员从混乱的情绪中看见关系事实。它记录的不只是“谁说了什么”，还包括“谁被忽视了”“哪些伤害反复发生”“哪些表达带来了改善”“哪些行动真正降低了冲突”。

在家庭文明工程中，文明记录是关系修复的重要工具。没有记录，许多家庭会不断陷入同样的争吵，事后又各执一词。文明记录让关系问题从情绪泥潭进入可观察、可复盘、可改进的工程过程。

## English Definition

Civilized Recording refers to documenting family communication, conflict review, relational repair, and growth practices in a way that respects facts, feelings, and boundaries, rather than recording others for the purpose of attack, accusation, judgment, or control.

Its purpose is not to collect evidence to punish someone, but to help family members see relational facts amid emotional chaos. It records not only “who said what,” but also “who was ignored,” “which harms repeatedly occurred,” “which expressions led to improvement,” and “which actions truly reduced conflict.”

Within the Family Civilization Project, Civilized Recording is an important tool for relational repair. Without records, many families repeatedly fall into the same arguments and later hold conflicting memories. Civilized Recording turns relational problems from an emotional swamp into an observable, reviewable, and improvable engineering process.
