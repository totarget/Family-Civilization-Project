# 033. Victim Fixation / 被害者执念

## 定义 / Definition

**被害者执念**，是指一个人在真实受过伤害之后，长期把自我身份、关系解释和人生意义固定在“我是被害者”这一位置上，以至于无法继续发展行动能力、创造能力、爱与被爱的能力。

**Victim fixation** refers to a pattern in which a person, after real harm, remains fixed in the identity, relational interpretation, and life meaning of “I am the victim,” to the point that the capacity for action, creation, loving, and being loved is unable to continue developing.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

被害者执念不等于承认受害事实。承认受害事实是疗愈的开始；被害者执念则是生命长期停留在伤害现场。它会使人不断证明自己受过伤，却难以走向新的生活。

Victim fixation is not the same as acknowledging victimization. Acknowledging harm is the beginning of healing; victim fixation keeps life at the site of harm. It leads a person to repeatedly prove that harm occurred, while finding it difficult to move toward a new life.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明必须同时承认伤害的真实存在，也帮助受伤者从伤害中重新获得生命主权。真正的修复，不是永远停留在控诉，而是让伤口不再继续支配自己、关系和下一代。

Family civilization must both acknowledge the reality of harm and help the wounded regain sovereignty of life. True repair does not remain forever in accusation; it allows the wound to stop governing the self, relationships, and the next generation.
