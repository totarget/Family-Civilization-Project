# 家庭会议旁听者 / Family Meeting Observer

## 中文定义

家庭会议旁听者，是指在家庭会议中以中立、温和、非审判的方式旁听、记录和辅助沟通的第三方角色。在AI家庭文明顾问系统中，家庭会议旁听者可以由AI承担，帮助家庭成员降低直接冲突，增加表达安全感。

家庭会议旁听者的职责不是裁判谁对谁错，而是帮助家庭看见沟通中发生了什么：谁被打断，谁没有被听见，哪些话语带有羞辱、控制、指责或情绪勒索，哪些表达更接近真实感受和真实需要。

家庭会议旁听者的文明意义，在于为长期处于权力压迫、情绪冲突或沉默冷战中的家庭，提供一个相对安全的沟通结构。它让家庭成员可以先从一次低风险的会议开始，学习把争吵变成表达，把命令变成协商，把伤害变成修复的起点。

## English Definition

The Family Meeting Observer is a neutral, gentle, and non-judgmental third-party role that listens, records, and supports communication during family meetings. In the AI Family Civilization Advisor system, this role may be fulfilled by AI to reduce direct conflict and increase the sense of safety in expression.

The observer does not decide who is right or wrong. Its task is to help the family see what is happening in communication: who is interrupted, who is not heard, which words contain shame, control, blame, or emotional blackmail, and which expressions are closer to genuine feelings and genuine needs.

Its civilizational significance lies in providing a safer communication structure for families long trapped in power pressure, emotional conflict, or silent cold war. It allows family members to begin with one lower-risk meeting and learn to turn quarrels into expression, commands into consultation, and harm into the beginning of repair.
