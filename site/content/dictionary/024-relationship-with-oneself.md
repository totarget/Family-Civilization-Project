# 024. Relationship with Oneself / 与自己的关系

## 定义 / Definition

**与自己的关系**，是指一个人如何看待、理解、对待并安放自己的身体、情绪、过去、伤口、价值、尊严、欲望、边界和未来。

它不是孤立的自我关注，也不是脱离他人的自恋，而是一切关系的内在原型。一个人如何对待自己，往往会深刻影响他如何对待他人，也影响他允许他人如何对待自己。

**Relationship with oneself** refers to the way a person sees, understands, treats, and holds their body, emotions, past, wounds, value, dignity, desires, boundaries, and future.

It is not isolated self-absorption or narcissism detached from others. It is the inner prototype of all relationships. The way a person relates to the self profoundly shapes how they relate to others, and what forms of treatment they allow from others.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

在家庭文明视角中，外部关系的质量，往往建立在内部关系的质量之上。

一个人若长期以羞辱、否定、压抑、忽视、攻击的方式对待自己，就很难真正建立稳定、自由、文明的外部关系。相反，当一个人逐渐学会看见自己、照顾自己、尊重自己、安抚自己、保护自己，他才更可能以成熟的方式爱别人，也更能拒绝不文明的关系。

From the perspective of family civilization, the quality of external relationships often rests on the quality of inner relationship.

When a person constantly relates to the self through shame, denial, suppression, neglect, or attack, it becomes difficult to build stable, free, and civilized relationships with others. When a person learns to see, care for, respect, soothe, and protect the self, they become more capable of loving others maturely and refusing uncivilized relationships.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明不仅要求父母善待孩子，也要求已经受伤的成年人重新学习善待自己。

许多成年人的关系痛苦，表面发生在伴侣、父母、孩子之间，深层却源于其内在仍然没有建立文明的自我关系。与自己的关系被修复之后，一个人才可能停止把自己的爱的缺口、恐惧和未完成的童年转嫁给他人。

Family civilization not only asks parents to treat children well; it also asks wounded adults to relearn how to treat themselves well.

Many adult relational sufferings appear to take place between partners, parents, and children, but at a deeper level they often arise from the absence of a civilized relationship with oneself. Once this inner relationship is repaired, a person becomes more able to stop transferring love deficits, fears, and unfinished childhood wounds onto others.
