# 048. Adult Children Facing Family of Origin / 成年子女面对原生家庭

## 定义 / Definition

**成年子女面对原生家庭**，成年子女面对原生家庭，是指已经成年的子女在承认早年伤害、保护自身边界、处理亲情期待、决定联系距离与重建自我关系的过程中，以清醒、尊严和责任重新面对父母及原生家庭系统。

**Adult Children Facing Family of Origin**: Adult children facing the family of origin refers to the process by which grown children confront their parents and original family system with clarity, dignity, and responsibility while acknowledging early harm, protecting boundaries, dealing with family expectations, deciding relational distance, and rebuilding the relationship with themselves.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

面对原生家庭，不等于无条件原谅，也不等于永远仇恨；不等于必须回家，也不等于必须断联。它要求成年人把自己从旧有的恐惧、内疚、讨好和被害者执念中逐渐带出来，重新拥有生命主权。

Facing the family of origin does not mean unconditional forgiveness, nor does it mean endless hatred; it does not require returning home, nor does it require no contact. It asks adults to gradually recover themselves from fear, guilt, people-pleasing, and victim fixation, and to reclaim sovereignty of life.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明为成年子女提供的不是仇恨叙事，而是修复路径：看见伤害，保护自己，停止转嫁，必要时保持边界，并在可能的地方重建关系。
