# 032. Sovereignty of Life / 生命主权

## 定义 / Definition

**生命主权**，是指一个人对自身生命价值、人生方向、情绪责任、关系选择和行动能力的基本拥有权。

它意味着一个人的人生不能被父母、伴侣、孩子、社会评价、过去创伤或外部权威完全支配。

**Sovereignty of life** refers to a person’s basic ownership over the value of their life, direction of life, emotional responsibility, relational choices, and capacity for action.

It means that one’s life should not be completely governed by parents, partners, children, social judgment, past wounds, or external authority.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

失去生命主权的人，容易把幸福交给别人，把价值交给别人，把选择交给别人，把过去创伤当成未来命运。重建生命主权，就是重新确认：人生可以受过去影响，但不必永远由过去决定。

A person who loses sovereignty of life tends to hand happiness, value, choices, and future destiny over to others or to past wounds. Rebuilding sovereignty means recognizing that life may be influenced by the past, but need not be forever determined by it.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明反对以爱、孝顺、血缘、牺牲、感恩之名剥夺一个人的生命主权。无论父母还是孩子，每个人都应当被作为拥有独立生命的人来对待。

Family civilization opposes depriving a person of the sovereignty of life in the name of love, filial piety, blood ties, sacrifice, or gratitude. Whether parent or child, every person should be treated as a human being with an independent life.
