# 文明入口 / Civilizational Entrance

## 中文定义

文明入口，是指一个人、一个家庭或一个共同体从旧有伤害模式进入文明实践的最初通道。它不要求所有人一开始就完成觉醒，而是提供一个足够温和、具体、可尝试的开始。

在家庭文明工程中，一次家庭会议、一次AI家庭文明顾问咨询、一次安全表达、一次停止羞辱、一次真正道歉，都可以成为文明入口。文明入口的意义，是让改变不再显得遥远、抽象和沉重，而是从一个可以做到的小行动开始。

文明入口体现了家庭文明工程的实践精神：文明不是等所有人都准备好才开始，而是通过一个低门槛、低冲突、可重复的小行动，让新的关系秩序逐渐出现。

## English Definition

A Civilizational Entrance refers to the initial passage through which a person, family, or community moves from old patterns of harm into civilizational practice. It does not require everyone to complete awakening at the beginning, but provides a sufficiently gentle, concrete, and tryable start.

Within the Family Civilization Project, one family meeting, one AI Family Civilization Advisor consultation, one safe expression, one cessation of humiliation, or one genuine apology can become a civilizational entrance. Its meaning lies in making change no longer seem distant, abstract, or heavy, but able to begin from one small action that can actually be done.

The concept embodies the practical spirit of Family Civilization Engineering: civilization does not begin only after everyone is ready. It begins through a low-threshold, low-conflict, repeatable small action that gradually allows a new relational order to appear.
