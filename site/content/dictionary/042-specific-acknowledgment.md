# 042. Specific Acknowledgment / 具体承认

## 定义 / Definition

**具体承认**，具体承认，是指在道歉或关系修复中，不以笼统、模糊、逃避性的语言概括过去，而是清楚说出具体造成伤害的行为、场景和影响。

**Specific Acknowledgment**: Specific acknowledgment means that in apology or relational repair, one does not hide behind vague and evasive language, but clearly names the specific behavior, situation, and impact that caused harm.

## 核心机制 / Core Mechanism

“我以前做得不好”常常不足以修复关系。具体承认需要说出：“我曾经羞辱你”“我曾经侵犯你的隐私”“我曾经把自己的焦虑压到你身上”“我曾经没有认真听你说话”。只有伤害被具体命名，受伤者才可能感到自己的痛苦被真正看见。

“I did not do well before” is often insufficient for repair. Specific acknowledgment requires statements such as: “I humiliated you,” “I invaded your privacy,” “I placed my anxiety on you,” or “I failed to truly listen to you.” Only when harm is specifically named can the wounded person feel that their pain has truly been seen.

## 家庭文明意义 / Meaning for Family Civilization

家庭文明反对用抽象亲情掩盖具体伤害。具体承认使家庭从模糊的情绪纠缠进入清晰的责任结构。
