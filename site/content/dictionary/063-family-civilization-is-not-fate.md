# 家庭文明不是命运 / Family Civilization Is Not Fate

## 中文定义

家庭文明不是命运，是指一个人的原生家庭、早年伤害、父母模式和代际创伤虽然会深刻影响人生，但并不决定一个人和一个家庭的最终走向。家庭关系不是只能忍受、重复或断裂，也可以被理解、修复、重建和优化。

这一概念反对宿命论。家庭伤害真实存在，原生家庭的影响也不可轻视，但家庭文明工程强调：人可以通过觉知、行动、边界、修复和新的关系实践，逐渐改变旧有模式。

家庭文明不是命运，意味着过去可以被看见，但不必永远统治未来；伤口可以被承认，但不必继续传递；家庭可以从创伤系统，逐渐转变为文明系统。

## English Definition

Family Civilization Is Not Fate means that one’s family of origin, early wounds, parental patterns, and intergenerational trauma may deeply influence life, but they do not determine the final direction of a person or a family. Family relationships are not only to be endured, repeated, or broken; they can also be understood, repaired, rebuilt, and optimized.

This concept opposes fatalism. Family harm is real, and the influence of the family of origin should not be underestimated. Yet the Family Civilization Project emphasizes that people can gradually change old patterns through awareness, action, boundaries, repair, and new relational practices.

It means that the past can be seen without ruling the future forever; wounds can be acknowledged without being passed on; and families can gradually move from systems of trauma to systems of civilization.
