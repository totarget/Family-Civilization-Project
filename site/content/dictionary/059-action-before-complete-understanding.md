# 行先于知 / Action Before Complete Understanding

## 中文定义

行先于知，是指在复杂的家庭关系修复中，不必等待所有人先完全理解理论、完全放下防御、完全完成觉醒，才开始实践。相反，适当的行动可以先发生，新的理解可以从行动结果中逐渐生长出来。

这并不否定认知的重要性，而是承认现实中的家庭改变往往不能只依靠说理完成。很多父母不愿读书，很多孩子不愿再解释，很多夫妻一开口就争吵。在这种情况下，最重要的不是先说服所有人，而是设计一个足够低风险、足够温和、足够具体的行动，让关系先出现一点点不同。

行先于知，是家庭文明工程区别于单纯理论书的重要原则。文明不是只被解释出来的，也是被实践出来的；人不是只因懂得而改变，也常常是在行动中看见新的可能，才逐渐愿意理解。

## English Definition

Action Before Complete Understanding means that in complex family repair, it is not necessary for everyone to fully understand the theory, completely lower their defenses, or fully awaken before practice begins. Instead, appropriate action can begin first, and new understanding can gradually grow from the results of action.

This does not deny the importance of understanding. It recognizes that real family change often cannot be achieved through reasoning alone. Many parents are unwilling to read books, many children no longer want to explain, and many couples quarrel as soon as they speak. In such situations, the most important thing is not to persuade everyone first, but to design a sufficiently low-risk, gentle, and concrete action that allows the relationship to become slightly different.

Action Before Complete Understanding is an important principle that distinguishes Family Civilization Engineering from a purely theoretical book. Civilization is not only explained; it is practiced. People do not change only because they understand; often they are willing to understand only after action reveals new possibilities.
