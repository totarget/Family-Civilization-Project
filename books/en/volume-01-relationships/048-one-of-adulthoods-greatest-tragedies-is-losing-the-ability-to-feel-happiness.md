# Section XLVIII: One of Adulthood’s Greatest Tragedies Is Losing the Ability to Feel Happiness

Section XLVIII: One of Adulthood’s Greatest Tragedies Is Losing the Ability to Feel Happiness
Many adults are not unsuccessful.
In fact,
many have already achieved:
Careers.
Income.
Families.
Social status.
Some have even reached standards others deeply admire.
Yet internally they still carry one persistent feeling:
Emptiness.
As though life has lost its living quality.
And future civilization will increasingly recognize:
One of modern humanity’s deepest crises is not lacking more things.
It is gradually losing the ability to feel happiness itself.

---

I. Many People Never Truly Experienced Relaxed Joy During Childhood
This is one of modern personality crisis’s deepest roots.
Many children grow up surrounded by:
Pressure.
Comparison.
Control.
Expectation.
Competition.
Thus life becomes organized around:
“You must work harder.”
“You must succeed.”
“You must become exceptional.”
As a result many children learn very early:
How to feel anxiety.
But never learn:
How to peacefully experience life.
Even while playing,
many children quietly feel:
“I should not waste time.”
“I cannot fall behind.”
“I must not disappoint my parents.”
And gradually personality loses:
Ease.

---

II. Personality Living Under Constant Pressure Gradually Loses Emotional Sensitivity
Future psychological civilization will increasingly recognize:
Human beings trapped for long periods inside:
Stress,
fear,
anxiety,
and self-suppression
gradually enter:
Survival mode.
And when personality remains in survival mode too long,
the first thing often lost is:
Sensitivity to life itself.
Thus many people eventually struggle to truly feel:
Joy.
Peace.
Wonder.
Love.
Happiness.
Because personality becomes reduced to:
Functional operation.

---

III. Many Modern Human Beings Are Gradually Becoming High-Functioning Machines
This is one of future civilization’s most urgent problems.
Many adults spend every day:
Working.
Managing tasks.
Maintaining efficiency.
Solving problems.
Yet increasingly rarely:
Experiencing life.
Many have not truly:
Watched a sunset quietly,
shared a peaceful meal,
felt the wind,
deeply accompanied loved ones,
or done something purely out of joy
for a very long time.
Because personality has been trained into:
A continuously operating functional system.
Thus many people survive without collapsing —
yet also without truly living.

---

IV. Truly Happy People Often Retain a Sense of Aliveness
Future civilization will increasingly realize:
Happiness is not merely possessing more.
It is:
Recovering the ability to feel life itself.
Meaning truly happy people still retain the capacity to:
Feel sunlight.
Feel connection.
Feel love.
Feel authenticity.
Feel emotional movement within themselves.
Thus happiness comes not only from outcomes,
but from:
Whether personality remains alive.

---

V. Many People Become Emotionally Numb Because Personality Was Never Allowed to Be Free
Future psychological civilization will increasingly recognize:
Chronically suppressed people gradually become:
Numb.
Because when human beings cannot:
Express authentically,
choose freely,
or become themselves,
personality slowly shuts down:
Emotional sensitivity.
This is often a psychological survival mechanism.
Thus many adults eventually become:
People who “feel nothing.”
Not because they are naturally cold,
but because personality has become:
Exhausted.

---

VI. Advanced Civilization Must Do More Than Increase Efficiency — It Must Help Humanity Feel Alive Again
This marks one of future civilization’s most important transformations.
Many historical civilizations emphasized:
Production.
Efficiency.
Competition.
Growth.
But future civilization will increasingly realize:
If human beings lose the ability to feel happiness, no amount of efficiency can truly create civilization.
Thus future advanced civilization will increasingly value:
Relationships.
Emotion.
Experience.
Inner well-being.
Quality of life.
Because:
Civilization’s true purpose is not merely helping people function more efficiently.
It is helping people feel alive again.

---

VII. The Family Civilization Project Ultimately Seeks to Help Humanity Recover the Feeling of Being Alive
One of the deepest goals of the Family Civilization Project is helping civilization restore:
Humanity’s capacity to feel life deeply again.
Because modern society has produced too many people who are:
Highly efficient.
Highly anxious.
Highly functional.
Deeply unhappy.
Many appear externally:
Mature.
Yet internally:
Exhausted.
Thus future civilized families must do more than produce competitive individuals.
They must help cultivate people who still retain the ability to:
Love life.
Feel connection.
Experience freedom.
Feel happiness.
Feel authentically alive.
Because:
Humanity’s greatest tragedy is not poverty.
It is succeeding outwardly while losing the ability to feel happiness inwardly.
And perhaps one of future civilization’s greatest missions is:
Helping human beings move from merely surviving
back toward truly living.
