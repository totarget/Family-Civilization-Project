## Publishing Note

This English edition is not a literal translation of the Chinese manual. It is an English-native adaptation for families in English-speaking contexts. It keeps the core principle of the Family Civilization Project while adjusting language, examples, and action steps for parents, adult children, couples, blended families, and households shaped by modern schools, digital life, therapy culture, co-parenting, elder care, privacy expectations, and individual life choices.

This manual is designed as an action dictionary. It is not meant to be read only from beginning to end. A reader may open the question that matches the family situation of the day, read the principle, use the suggested words, and try one small repair action.

This book does not replace emergency services, mental health care, medical care, legal support, child protection systems, domestic violence resources, or professional therapy. When safety is at risk, professional and local support must come first.

## Core Principle

**Human beings are ends, not means.**

A child is not a project.  
A parent is not a ruler.  
A partner is not property.  
An elder is not a burden.  
A family member is not a tool for someone else’s emotional needs.

Family Civilization begins when a family decides to reduce harm, protect dignity, build boundaries, take responsibility, and make repair possible.
