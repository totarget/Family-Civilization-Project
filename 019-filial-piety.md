# 019 | Filial Piety / 孝顺

## English Definition

Filial piety originally referred to an ethical relationship of care, responsibility, and intergenerational support within human civilization.

However, in many traditional family systems, filial piety gradually became distorted into absolute obedience to parents, suppression of personal boundaries, prohibition of disagreement, and enduring emotional harm in the name of family duty.

As a result, filial piety often shifted from a relationship of love into a structure of power and control.

Within modern relationship civilization, true filial responsibility should never require the destruction of one’s dignity, identity, or psychological well-being.

Healthy filial piety means that adult children may still choose to care for, respect, and support their parents while maintaining independent personhood, emotional boundaries, and mental health.

Filial piety is not limitless obedience. It is not self-erasure. It is not enduring ongoing abuse.

The essence of filial responsibility should not be submission to authority. It should be the continuation of love and responsibility within relationships of equal human dignity.

## 中文定义

“孝顺”，原本是人类文明中关于亲情、责任与代际关爱的伦理。

但在很多旧家庭结构中，“孝顺”逐渐异化为对父母绝对服从、不允许反抗、不允许建立边界、即使持续受伤也必须忍耐。

于是，“孝”开始从爱的关系，变成一种权力结构。

在现代关系文明中，真正的孝，不应建立在人格压迫之上。

健康的孝，应该是成年子女在保持独立人格、边界与精神健康的前提下，依然愿意对父母保持责任、关爱与尊重。

孝，不等于无限服从；孝，不等于失去自我；孝，不等于忍受持续伤害。

“孝”的本质，不应该是权力服从，而应该是爱与责任在人格平等中的延续。
