# 004. Children Are Ends, Not Tools
# 004. 孩子是目的，不是工具

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

Children are not born to complete the unfinished lives of their parents.

孩子不是为了完成父母未完成的人生而来到这个世界上的。

Children are not parental property.

Children are not family assets.

Children are not reputation projects.

Children are not emotional containers.

Children are not retirement tools.

孩子不是父母的私有物。

孩子不是家庭资产。

孩子不是面子工程。

孩子不是情绪容器。

孩子不是养老工具。

A child is first of all a human being.

孩子首先是一个人。

---

## The Silenced Child
## 沉默的孩子

Many children grow up without ever feeling that they are treated as real persons.

很多孩子长大过程中，从未真正感受过自己被当成一个人。

Their voices are not heard.

Their feelings are not seen.

Their pain is not believed.

Their boundaries are not respected.

Their dignity is not protected.

他们的声音没人听见。

他们的感受没人看见。

他们的痛苦没人相信。

他们的边界没人尊重。

他们的尊严没人保护。

This is why the Family Civilization Project must speak for wounded and silenced children.

这就是为什么《家庭文明工程》必须替受伤的、沉默无语的孩子发声。

---

## I Speak for Wounded and Silenced Children
## 我是受伤沉默孩子的代言人

The emotional positioning of the Family Civilization Project is:

> I speak for all wounded and silenced children.

> 我是所有受过伤害、沉默无语的孩子的代言人。

This does not mean creating hatred between children and parents.

这不是为了制造孩子与父母之间的仇恨。

It means restoring the dignity of the child.

而是为了恢复孩子作为人的尊严。

It means saying:

- I am hurt.
- I am wronged.
- I am in pain.
- I have dignity.
- I have feelings.
- I have boundaries.
- I am not your tool.
- I am your child, but I am first a person.

它意味着替孩子说：

- 我很受伤；
- 我很委屈；
- 我很痛苦；
- 我有尊严；
- 我有感受；
- 我有边界；
- 我不是你的工具；
- 我是你的孩子，但我首先是一个人。

---

## Core Sentence
## 核心句

> A child is your child, but first of all, a child is a person.

> 孩子是你的孩子，但首先，孩子是一个人。
