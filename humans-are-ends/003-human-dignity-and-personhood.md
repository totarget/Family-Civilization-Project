# 003. Human Dignity and Personhood
# 003. 人格尊严与做人权利

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

A human being does not become a person after becoming useful, obedient, successful, or mature.

A human being is a person from the beginning.

一个人不是在变得有用、听话、成功或成熟之后才成为人。

一个人从一开始就是人。

This is especially important for children.

这对孩子尤其重要。

---

## A Child Is a Person From Birth
## 孩子从出生起就是人

A child may be weak.

A child may be dependent.

A child may not yet speak.

A child may not yet understand the world.

孩子可能很弱小。

孩子可能需要依赖父母。

孩子可能还不会说话。

孩子可能还不理解世界。

But none of this means that the child has no dignity.

但这一切都不意味着孩子没有尊严。

Weakness is not inferiority.

Dependency is not ownership.

Immaturity is not permission for domination.

弱小不等于低等。

依赖不等于归属。

不成熟不等于可以被支配。

---

## Personhood in Family Relationships
## 家庭关系中的人格

Personhood means that a person has:

- feelings;
- dignity;
- boundaries;
- inner life;
- the right to be seen;
- the right not to be humiliated;
- the right not to be reduced to a tool.

人格意味着一个人拥有：

- 感受；
- 尊严；
- 边界；
- 内在生命；
- 被看见的权利；
- 不被羞辱的权利；
- 不被降低为工具的权利。

A child has personhood.

A partner has personhood.

A parent has personhood.

Every family member has personhood.

孩子有人格。

伴侣有人格。

父母有人格。

每一个家庭成员都有人格。

---

## Core Sentence
## 核心句

> A child does not become a person after becoming useful. A child is a person from the beginning.

> 孩子不是变得有用之后才成为人。孩子从一开始就是人。
