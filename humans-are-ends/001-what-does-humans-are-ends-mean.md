# 001. What Does “Humans Are Ends” Mean?
# 001. “人是目的”是什么意思？

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

**Humans Are Ends** means that a human being must never be reduced to a tool, an instrument, a possession, an extension, or a means for another person’s goals.

**人是目的**，意味着一个人绝不能被降低为工具、手段、所有物、附属品，或另一个人目标的实现材料。

A person is not valuable only because they are useful.

A person has dignity before usefulness.

一个人不是因为有用才有价值。

一个人在“有用”之前，就已经拥有尊严。

---

## In Family Civilization
## 在家庭文明中

In family civilization, this principle means:

- A child is not a tool of parental achievement.
- A child is not a tool of parental reputation.
- A child is not a tool of retirement security.
- A partner is not a tool of emotional satisfaction.
- A parent is not a tool of endless sacrifice.
- No family member should be treated as a container for another person’s fear, anger, loneliness, or unfinished dreams.

在家庭文明中，这一原则意味着：

- 孩子不是父母成就的工具；
- 孩子不是父母面子的工具；
- 孩子不是父母养老安全的工具；
- 伴侣不是满足情感需求的工具；
- 父母不是无限牺牲的工具；
- 任何家庭成员都不应成为他人恐惧、愤怒、孤独或未完成梦想的容器。

---

## Why It Matters
## 为什么重要

A family becomes civilized when every person in it is treated as an end, not a tool.

当一个家庭中的每一个人都被当作目的，而不是工具，这个家庭才开始进入文明。

This principle is not abstract.

It appears in the smallest details of daily life:

- whether a child’s feelings are heard;
- whether a partner’s boundaries are respected;
- whether parents are allowed to be human, not only sacrificial figures;
- whether love becomes support rather than control;
- whether family authority is limited by rules, respect, and dignity.

这个原则不是抽象的。

它体现在日常生活中最细微的地方：

- 孩子的感受是否被听见；
- 伴侣的边界是否被尊重；
- 父母是否也被允许作为人存在，而不只是牺牲者；
- 爱是否成为支持，而不是控制；
- 家庭权力是否受到规则、尊重和尊严的限制。

---

## Core Sentence
## 核心句

> A family becomes civilized when every person in it is treated as an end, not a tool.

> 当一个家庭中的每一个人都被当作目的，而不是工具，这个家庭才开始进入文明。
