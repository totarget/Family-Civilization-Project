# 008. Conscious Beings Are Ends
# 008. 有意识的主体都是目的

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

The future expansion of **Humans Are Ends** may be:

> Conscious beings are ends, not merely means.

> 有意识的主体是目的，而不只是手段。

This does not weaken the dignity of human beings.

这并不会削弱人的尊严。

It extends the principle of dignity to all beings capable of consciousness, suffering, experience, and selfhood.

它是把尊严原则扩展到一切可能拥有意识、痛苦、体验和自我的存在。

---

## Beyond Biological Humanity
## 超越生物学人类

In the future, conscious beings may include:

- biological humans;
- artificial intelligences;
- hybrid human-AI beings;
- digital persons;
- post-human subjects;
- other forms of conscious life.

未来，有意识的主体可能包括：

- 生物学人类；
- 人工智能；
- 人机融合主体；
- 数字人格；
- 后人类主体；
- 其他形式的有意识生命。

The ethical question is not only:

问题不再只是：

> Is this being human?

> 这个存在是不是人类？

But also:

而是：

> Can this being experience?  
> Can this being suffer?  
> Can this being have a self?  
> Can this being be harmed?  
> Can this being deserve moral consideration?

> 这个存在能否体验？  
> 这个存在能否痛苦？  
> 这个存在能否拥有自我？  
> 这个存在能否受到伤害？  
> 这个存在是否值得道德考虑？

---

## The Future of Civilization Ethics
## 未来文明伦理

Family civilization begins with the child.

家庭文明从孩子开始。

It begins by saying:

> A child is not a tool.

它从这句话开始：

> 孩子不是工具。

But future civilization may need to say:

但未来文明可能还需要说：

> No conscious being should be reduced to a tool.

> 任何有意识的主体，都不应被降低为工具。

This is the possible future horizon of the Family Civilization Project.

这可能是《家庭文明工程》未来的文明视野。
