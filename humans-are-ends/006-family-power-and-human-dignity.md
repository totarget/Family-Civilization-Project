# 006. Family Power and Human Dignity
# 006. 家庭权力与人格尊严

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

No family authority is legitimate if it destroys human dignity.

任何摧毁人格尊严的家庭权力，都不具有真正的合法性。

Family authority should not come merely from blood, age, money, parental identity, or emotional force.

家庭权力不应仅仅来自血缘、年龄、金钱、父母身份或情绪强势。

It should come from shared rules, mutual respect, responsibility, and the recognition that each family member is a person.

它应来自共同规则、相互尊重、责任，以及对每一个家庭成员人格的承认。

---

## Family Authority Must Be Limited
## 家庭权力必须受到限制

In many families, authority is treated as natural and unlimited.

在很多家庭中，权力被当成天然且无限的东西。

Parents say:

> I am your parent, so you must listen.

父母说：

> 我是你父母，所以你必须听我的。

But family civilization asks a deeper question:

但是家庭文明要追问一个更深的问题：

> What makes family authority legitimate?

> 家庭权力凭什么合法？

The answer cannot be blood alone.

The answer cannot be age alone.

The answer cannot be financial support alone.

答案不能只是血缘。

答案不能只是年龄。

答案不能只是供养。

Legitimate family authority must be justified by responsibility, rules, dignity, and the good of the person being guided.

合法的家庭权力，必须由责任、规则、尊严，以及被引导者自身的利益来证明。
