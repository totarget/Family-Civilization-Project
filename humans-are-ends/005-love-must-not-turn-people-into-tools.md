# 005. Love Must Not Turn People Into Tools
# 005. 爱不能把人变成工具

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

Love is one of the most precious human experiences.

爱是人类最珍贵的经验之一。

But love becomes uncivilized when it turns another person into an instrument of one’s own needs.

但当爱把另一个人变成满足自己需求的工具时，爱就失去了文明。

---

## Love Is Not Control
## 爱不是控制

Love is not control.

Love is not possession.

Love is not emotional blackmail.

Love is not the demand that another person live according to your expectations.

爱不是控制。

爱不是占有。

爱不是情感勒索。

爱不是要求另一个人按照你的期待生活。

When parents use love to control children, love becomes harm.

When partners use love to possess each other, love becomes harm.

When family members use love to demand obedience, love becomes harm.

当父母用爱控制孩子时，爱就变成伤害。

当伴侣用爱占有彼此时，爱就变成伤害。

当家人用爱要求服从时，爱就变成伤害。

---

## The Love-Harm Mechanism
## 爱的伤害机制

The Family Civilization Dictionary uses the concept **Love-Harm Mechanism** to describe how love becomes harm when it loses personhood, boundaries, respect, and emotional maturity.

《家庭文明词典》使用 **爱的伤害机制 / Love-Harm Mechanism** 来描述：当爱失去人格、边界、尊重和情绪成熟时，爱如何变成伤害。

Love itself does not harm people.

What harms people is love mixed with control, possession, fear, dependency, humiliation, and boundary violation.

爱本身并不伤人。

真正伤人的，是爱与控制、占有、恐惧、依赖、羞辱和边界侵犯混合在一起。

---

## Core Sentence
## 核心句

> Love becomes uncivilized when it turns another person into an instrument of one’s own needs.

> 当爱把另一个人变成满足自己需求的工具时，爱就失去了文明。
