# HUMANS ARE ENDS

## Philosophy Column of the Family Civilization Project

This English directory contains the English edition of the **HUMANS ARE ENDS** philosophy column.

The purpose of this column is to tell, in a readable and story-based way, how human civilization gradually moved toward one of its most important moral principles:

> Human beings are ends, not means.

This principle is not only a sentence from moral philosophy. It is also the foundation of family civilization.

In the family, a child must not be treated as a tool for parental pride, academic competition, retirement security, emotional compensation, or unfinished dreams.

A spouse must not be treated as a tool for emotional dumping, economic extraction, social appearance, or personal control.

Parents, too, must not be reduced to endless providers, emotional containers, or instruments of family obligation.

A civilized family begins when each person is seen as a person.

## Editorial Style

The English version should be native, clear, emotionally resonant, and readable for general readers. It should avoid excessive academic jargon.

The goal is not to teach philosophy as a school subject, but to show how philosophy can help modern families reduce harm, protect dignity, and rebuild relationships.
