# Resources for HUMANS ARE ENDS

This resources file lists the major philosophical sources and themes that support the HUMANS ARE ENDS column.

The column is written for general readers, not as an academic textbook. Its purpose is to connect philosophy with family civilization, dignity, parent-child relationships, intimate relationships, and AI ethics.

## Ancient Greek Philosophy

### Socrates

Core themes:

- The examined life
- Moral questioning
- Care for the soul
- The courage to question inherited habits

Suggested sources:

- Plato, *Apology*
- Plato, *Crito*
- Plato, *Phaedo*
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Socrates

### Plato

Core themes:

- Justice
- The soul
- Order and disorder
- The danger of tyranny
- The relationship between the individual and the city

Suggested sources:

- Plato, *Republic*
- Plato, *Gorgias*
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Plato

### Aristotle

Core themes:

- Human flourishing
- Virtue
- Habit
- Practical wisdom
- Character formation

Suggested sources:

- Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics*
- Aristotle, *Politics*
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Aristotle’s Ethics

### Stoicism

Core themes:

- Inner freedom
- Moral dignity
- Emotional discipline
- Cosmopolitanism
- The person beyond external status

Suggested sources:

- Epictetus, *Enchiridion*
- Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations*
- Seneca, *Letters from a Stoic*
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Stoicism
