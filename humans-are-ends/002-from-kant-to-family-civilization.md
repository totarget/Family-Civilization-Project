# 002. From Kant to Family Civilization
# 002. 从康德到家庭文明

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

The principle **“Human beings are ends, not merely means”** is most famously associated with Immanuel Kant.

“人是目的，而不只是手段”这一原则，最著名地与伊曼努尔·康德联系在一起。

For Kant, human beings possess dignity because they are rational moral agents. They must never be treated merely as instruments for another person’s will.

对康德而言，人拥有尊严，是因为人是具有理性与道德能力的主体。人绝不能仅仅被当成他人意志的工具。

---

## From Philosophy to Family Life
## 从哲学到家庭生活

The Family Civilization Project brings this principle back into the most ordinary and most important place:

**the family.**

《家庭文明工程》要把这一原则带回最日常、也最重要的地方：

**家庭。**

Many people accept dignity in public life but abandon it inside family life.

许多人在公共生活中承认人的尊严，却在家庭生活中抛弃它。

They believe children should obey because they are children.

They believe parents can control because they are parents.

They believe partners may possess each other because they are in love.

他们认为，孩子因为是孩子，所以必须服从。

他们认为，父母因为是父母，所以可以控制。

他们认为，伴侣因为相爱，所以可以占有彼此。

Family civilization rejects this.

家庭文明拒绝这一点。

---

## Family Civilization as Daily Practice
## 家庭文明作为日常实践

Kant gave humanity a philosophical principle.

Family civilization turns it into daily relational practice.

康德给了人类一个哲学原则。

家庭文明要把它变成日常关系实践。

This means:

- Do not use love as a reason to control.
- Do not use family identity as a reason to dominate.
- Do not use sacrifice as a reason to morally blackmail.
- Do not use parental authority as a reason to erase a child’s personhood.
- Do not use intimacy as a reason to violate another person’s boundaries.

这意味着：

- 不要用爱作为控制的理由；
- 不要用家庭身份作为支配的理由；
- 不要用牺牲作为道德绑架的理由；
- 不要用父母权威作为抹除孩子人格的理由；
- 不要用亲密关系作为侵犯他人边界的理由。

---

## Core Sentence
## 核心句

> Kant gave humanity a philosophical principle. Family civilization turns it into daily relational practice.

> 康德给了人类一个哲学原则。家庭文明要把它变成日常关系实践。
