# 009. A Brief History of the Idea
# 009. “人是目的”的思想史

## Purpose of This File
## 本文件目的

This file will gradually collect the historical background of the idea that human beings possess dignity and should not be reduced to tools.

本文件将逐步整理“人拥有尊严，不应被降低为工具”这一思想的历史背景。

It is not intended to be completed all at once.

它不需要一次完成。

It is a growing archive for future writing, education, lectures, and AI-assisted research.

它是一个不断增长的档案，用于未来写作、教育、演讲和 AI 辅助研究。
