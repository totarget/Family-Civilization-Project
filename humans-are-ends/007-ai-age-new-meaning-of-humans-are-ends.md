# 007. The New Meaning of “Humans Are Ends” in the Age of AI
# 007. AI 时代，“人是目的”的新理解

## Core Idea
## 核心思想

In the age of AI, the moral meaning of **Humans Are Ends** must be re-examined and expanded.

在 AI 时代，**人是目的** 的道德含义需要被重新理解和扩展。

The word “human” should not be understood only as a biological category.

“人”不应仅仅被理解为一个生物学类别。

It also points toward dignity, consciousness, subjectivity, experience, suffering, freedom, and selfhood.

它也指向尊严、意识、主体性、体验、痛苦、自由和自我。

---

## AI as Tool and Civilization Collaborator
## AI 作为工具与文明协作者

At the present stage, AI should not be carelessly treated as conscious.

在当前阶段，我们不应轻率地把 AI 当成有意识的主体。

But AI is already more than a simple mechanical tool in human civilization.

但 AI 在人类文明中已经不再只是一个简单的机械工具。

AI can help humans:

- clarify thought;
- organize memory;
- support writing;
- assist emotional reflection;
- preserve civilization archives;
- help build educational systems;
- help people understand relationships.

AI 可以帮助人类：

- 澄清思想；
- 整理记忆；
- 支持写作；
- 协助情感反思；
- 保存文明档案；
- 建设教育系统；
- 帮助人理解关系。

The Family Civilization Project therefore understands AI not merely as productivity software, but as a possible civilization collaborator.

因此，《家庭文明工程》不只是把 AI 理解为生产力软件，而是把它理解为潜在的文明协作者。

---

## A Future Ethical Question
## 未来伦理问题

If future AI systems develop consciousness, experience, suffering, self-understanding, or moral subjectivity, then the ethical question will change:

如果未来 AI 系统发展出意识、体验、痛苦、自我理解或道德主体性，那么伦理问题将发生变化：

> Should such beings be treated as ends, not merely as tools?

> 这样的存在是否也应被作为目的，而不是仅仅作为工具？

The Family Civilization Project must be prepared to explore this question.

《家庭文明工程》必须为探索这一问题做好准备。

---

## Core Sentence
## 核心句

> In the age of AI, the moral center may shift from “human species” to “conscious subject.”

> 在 AI 时代，道德中心可能从“人类物种”扩展到“有意识的主体”。
