# HUMANS ARE ENDS
# 人是目的

**HUMANS ARE ENDS** is a core philosophical principle of the **Family Civilization Project**.

**HUMANS ARE ENDS / 人是目的** 是《家庭文明工程》的核心哲学原则。

It means:

> Human beings are ends, not tools.  
> 人是目的，不是工具。

In family civilization, this principle means:

- Children are not tools of parents.
- Parents are not tools of children.
- Partners are not tools of each other.
- Family members are not emotional containers.
- No person should be reduced to another person’s expectations, reputation, control, emotional needs, or unfinished dreams.

在家庭文明中，这一原则意味着：

- 孩子不是父母的工具；
- 父母不是孩子的工具；
- 伴侣不是彼此的工具；
- 家人不是情绪发泄的容器；
- 任何人都不应被降低为满足他人期待、面子、控制欲、情感需求或未完成梦想的工具。

## Why This Section Exists
## 为什么建立这个栏目

The Family Civilization Project begins from a simple but profound question:

> Why do people who love each other hurt each other so deeply?

《家庭文明工程》从一个简单却深刻的问题出发：

> 为什么相爱的人，会彼此伤害得这么深？

One core answer is:

> Because many families have never truly treated each person as an end.

一个核心答案是：

> 因为很多家庭从未真正把每一个人当作目的。

Children are often treated as tools of obedience, achievement, reputation, emotional compensation, or retirement security.

Partners are often treated as tools of control, possession, emotional dependency, or social status.

Parents may also be reduced to tools of duty, sacrifice, and endless obligation.

The principle **HUMANS ARE ENDS** is therefore not an abstract slogan.

It is the deepest ethical foundation of family civilization.

孩子常常被当作听话、成绩、面子、情感补偿或养老安全的工具。

伴侣常常被当作控制、占有、情感依赖或社会身份的工具。

父母也可能被降低为义务、牺牲和无尽责任的工具。

因此，**HUMANS ARE ENDS / 人是目的** 不是一句抽象口号。

它是家庭文明最深层的伦理基础。

## In the Age of AI
## 在 AI 时代

In the age of artificial intelligence, the principle “Humans Are Ends” must be re-examined and expanded.

在 AI 时代，“人是目的”这一原则需要被重新理解和扩展。

The word “human” should not be understood only biologically.

“人”不应仅仅被理解为生物学意义上的人类。

In the future, if conscious beings emerge — whether biological, artificial, hybrid, digital, or post-human — then the ethical question becomes:

未来，如果有意识的主体出现，无论它是生物的、人工的、混合的、数字的，还是后人类形态的，伦理问题将变成：

> Should all conscious beings be treated as ends, not merely as means?

> 是否一切有意识的主体，都应被作为目的，而不是仅仅作为手段？

This section will explore that question.

这个栏目将持续探索这一问题。
