# HUMANS ARE ENDS / 人是目的

This directory is the philosophy column of the Family Civilization Project.

本目录为《家庭文明工程 / Family Civilization Project》的哲学基础栏目。

Its purpose is to trace, in a readable and story-based way, the long philosophical path toward the principle:

> Human beings are ends, not means.  
> 人是目的，不是工具。

## Directory Structure / 目录结构

```text
humans-are-ends/
├── zh/    Chinese version / 中文版本
└── en/    English version / 英文版本
```

The Chinese and English versions are separated as independent language editions. They do not have to be word-for-word translations, but they should share the same intellectual direction, numbering, and core concepts.

中文与英文版本分开存档。两种语言不必逐字对应，但应保持相同的思想方向、编号体系与核心概念。

## Editorial Direction / 写作方向

This column should not become a dry academic history of philosophy. It should tell the story of how human beings gradually discovered dignity, conscience, freedom, personhood, and the moral danger of reducing people to tools.

本栏目不应写成枯燥的学院式哲学史，而应讲述人类如何逐步发现尊严、良知、自由、人格，以及把人降格为工具的道德危险。

The final destination of this column is not abstract theory, but family life: parent-child relationships, intimate relationships, family power, education, dignity, boundaries, and AI ethics.

本栏目的最终归宿不是抽象理论，而是家庭生活：亲子关系、亲密关系、家庭权力、教育、尊严、边界与 AI 伦理。
