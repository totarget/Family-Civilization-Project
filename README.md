# Family Civilization Project
## 家庭文明工程 / Family Civilization Project

**Family Civilization Project** is a long-term open civilization project dedicated to rebuilding family relationships, human dignity, healthy personality, emotional safety, relationship civilization, and AI-assisted human flourishing.

**家庭文明工程** 是一个长期开放的文明工程，致力于重建家庭关系、人格尊严、健康人格、情感安全、关系文明，以及 AI 辅助的人类幸福系统。

It begins with the family, but it does not stop at the family.

它从家庭开始，但并不止于家庭。

Its ultimate concern is the future of human relationships, human dignity, love, freedom, happiness, and the ethical development of civilization in the age of AI.

它最终关心的是：人类关系、人格尊严、爱、自由、幸福，以及 AI 时代文明伦理的发展。

---

## Core Principle / 核心原则

> **Human beings are ends, not tools.**  
> **人是目的，不是工具。**

Children are not tools of parents.  
Partners are not tools of each other.  
Parents are not tools of endless sacrifice.  
Ordinary people are not tools of systems.  
AI should never turn human beings into tools.

孩子不是父母的工具。  
伴侣不是彼此的工具。  
父母不是无限牺牲的工具。  
普通人不是系统的工具。  
AI 也不应该把人变成工具。

The highest purpose of civilization is to help every person live with dignity, freedom, love, meaning, and happiness.

文明的最高目的，是帮助每一个人获得尊严、自由、爱、意义和幸福。

## Mission / 使命

The mission of the Family Civilization Project is:

1. To help families move from control to respect.
2. To help parents stop hurting children in the name of love.
3. To help children grow into complete human beings with dignity, boundaries, emotional health, and independent ability.
4. To help couples rebuild relationships based on equality, respect, responsibility, and love.
5. To help wounded adults understand and heal the child within.
6. To build an open, evolving, AI-assisted civilization system for healthier human relationships.

家庭文明工程的使命是：

1. 帮助家庭从控制走向尊重。
2. 帮助父母停止以爱的名义伤害孩子。
3. 帮助孩子成长为有人格、有边界、有情感能力、有独立能力的完整的人。
4. 帮助夫妻重建基于平等、尊重、责任和爱的关系。
5. 帮助受伤的成年人理解并疗愈内心那个孩子。
6. 建立一个开放、进化、AI 辅助的关系文明系统。

## Why This Project Exists / 为什么要建立这个工程

Human civilization has created powerful technologies, industries, wealth, and artificial intelligence.

人类已经创造了强大的科技、工业、财富和人工智能。

But many people still suffer deeply inside families and intimate relationships.

但许多人依然在家庭和亲密关系中承受深重痛苦。

Many families are still built on control, fear, obedience, emotional suppression, moral blackmail, and intergenerational trauma.

许多家庭依然建立在控制、恐惧、服从、情感压抑、道德绑架和代际创伤之上。

Many people love one another, yet still hurt one another deeply.

许多人彼此相爱，却仍然彼此深深伤害。

The Family Civilization Project believes that the next stage of civilization must include relationship civilization.

家庭文明工程相信，人类文明的下一阶段，必须包括关系文明。

And the starting point of relationship civilization is the family.

而关系文明的起点，就是家庭。

## Key Concepts / 核心概念

### Humans Are Ends / 人是目的

Human beings must never be reduced to tools, means, possessions, extensions, emotional containers, or instruments for another person’s expectations.

人绝不能被降低为工具、手段、所有物、附属品、情绪容器，或他人期待的实现材料。

In the age of AI, this principle may need to expand toward a deeper ethical horizon:

在 AI 时代，这一原则可能需要扩展到更深的伦理视野：

> Human beings — and all conscious beings — are ends, not merely means.

> 人类，以及未来一切有意识的主体，都不应仅仅被作为工具。

### Love-Harm Mechanism / 爱的伤害机制

Love itself does not harm people.

爱本身不会伤人。

What turns love into harm is the absence of personhood, boundaries, respect, emotional maturity, and the capacity to love.

缺乏人格、边界、尊重、情绪成熟和爱的能力，才会把爱变成伤害。

### Family Authority Mechanism / 家庭权力机制

Family authority should not come merely from blood, age, financial support, parental identity, or emotional force.

家庭权力不应仅仅来自血缘、年龄、供养、父母身份或情绪强势。

Legitimate family authority must be rebuilt upon shared rules, responsibility, dignity, and mutual respect.

合法的家庭权力，必须建立在共同规则、责任、尊严和相互尊重之上。
