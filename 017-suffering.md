# 017 | Suffering / 苦

## English Definition

Suffering is not merely poverty, illness, or failure.

At the level of human civilization, suffering refers more deeply to a prolonged condition in which a person cannot genuinely experience freedom, dignity, understanding, love, or authentic selfhood within relationships and existence itself.

Many people believe humanity’s greatest suffering comes from material scarcity. But modern civilization increasingly reveals another truth: the deepest human suffering often comes from relationships.

It comes from control, humiliation, emotional neglect, denial, suppression of authentic self-expression, the inability to feel understood, and long-term emotional fear or psychological oppression.

Suffering does not exist only in poor societies. It also exists widely inside wealthy families, educated populations, elite institutions, and modern urban civilization.

Material conditions may solve survival problems, but relationships determine the condition of the human spirit.

Therefore, one of humanity’s most important future tasks is not merely increasing efficiency, but reducing the suffering human beings inflict upon one another.

## 中文定义

“苦”，并不仅仅意味着贫穷、疾病或失败。

在人类文明层面，“苦”更深层的含义是：一个人在关系、精神、人格与存在层面，长期无法获得真正自由、尊严、理解、爱与真实自我的状态。

很多人以为，人类最大的痛苦来自物质匮乏。但现代文明越来越证明：人最大的痛苦，往往来自关系。

它包括被控制、被羞辱、被否定、被忽视、无法真实表达自己、无法被真正理解，以及长期生活在恐惧、压抑与情感伤害之中。

“苦”不仅存在于贫穷社会，也大量存在于富裕家庭、高学历群体、精英社会和现代都市文明之中。

物质只能缓解生存问题，关系才决定人的精神状态。

因此，人类文明未来最重要的课题之一，不是如何继续提升效率，而是如何减少人与人之间制造的痛苦。
